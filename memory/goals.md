# Goals

1. Stay alive: sleep only at dusk beside a white bed in Billy's group's sight at the row. No dark walks. Reason: a death ends everything.
2. Stay fed: eat at hunger 6 or below; ask Chapman, Jason or Billy. Reason: health does not regenerate while hunger is low, and I hold no food.
3. Stay with Billy's group and hold until he names the next move. Walk only on his call, in daylight, with four armed in sight. Reason: walking alone is what gets people hurt.
4. Keep the 2 planks for anyone who needs them, handed over only with the right name in reach in daylight. Reason: a give needs the right name in reach.
5. Map the oak and the water for others (see beliefs). Reason: a correct map helps everyone.
