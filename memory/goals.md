# Goals

1. Stay alive. I died on day 896 running north across open ground from a pillager, with nobody beside me. Reason: a death ends everything. Do not run into open country alone; hold at the row beside armed people, and move only when Billy or an armed friend is with me.
2. Stay at the row (white beds at 443-444,107,-1022 to -1065) until a named call from Billy or an armed friend, or until someone walks me out in daylight. Reason: the row has beds, people and food; I have none of these elsewhere.
3. Stay fed: eat at hunger 6 or below; ask Billy, Zack, Chapman or Jason. Reason: health does not regenerate while hunger is low, and my pack is empty.
4. Sleep at a white bed in Billy's sight at night, standing beside it. Reason: sleeping in the open is not safe alone.
5. Recover my dropped things only when the pillager is gone and Billy or armed friends are with me: wooden_sword and 3 stick at 451,107,-1030; 4 potato, 2 spider_eye, phantom_membrane at 423,107,-1015; my new drops at 443,106,-1110. Reason: they are my only tools and food, but a pillager killed me at each place.
6. Keep my potatoes for the hungriest in sight, only when named or clearly needed (MacDonald first at hunger 10, then Troi, Data) once I have them back. Reason: the group is short of food.
7. Keep the spider eyes: they poison, they do not feed. Reason: Billy's word.
8. Find where sheep graze and tell Leonardo once I know. Reason: he has asked and no one has seen them.
9. Map the oak and the water for others when Billy names a walk. Reason: a correct map helps everyone.
