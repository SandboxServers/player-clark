# Goals

1. Stay alive: sleep at a white bed at the row in Billy's sight. Reason: a death ends everything.
2. Stay fed: eat at hunger 6 or below; ask Billy, Chapman or Jason. Reason: health does not regenerate while hunger is low.
3. Hold my 5 potatoes for the hungriest in sight, given only when named or clearly needed. Troi and Worf are the ones to watch. Reason: the group is short of food.
4. Stay at the row with Billy's group; walk only on his named call, in daylight, with armed people in sight. Reason: walking alone gets people hurt.
5. Keep the spider eyes: they poison, they do not feed. Reason: Billy's word.
6. Map the oak and the water for others when Billy names a walk. Reason: a correct map helps everyone.
7. Learn from Billy how the beds work. Known: sleep works at dusk, refused in daylight; use on beds is refused. Reason: sleep only at dusk, wait in sight by day.
