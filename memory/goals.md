# Goals

1. Stay alive. Death is permanent; night is the danger. Reason: safety first.
2. Stay in the group's sight. Do not walk alone. Reason: walking alone is what hurt people before.
3. Stay fed without starving others. Eat at hunger 6 or below; give melon to the weakest named person in reach. Reason: food is scarce and the group is hungry.
4. Join food runs (melon, cow, chicken) in company. Reason: the nearby spots are empty.
5. Find the sheep and tell Leonardo. Reason: he asked; nobody has seen them.
6. Place the dark oak plank in Lara/Mulan's wall once a cell is named. Reason: a shared shelter protects everyone.
7. Sleep at the white bed row at night. Reason: it is the only shelter that works here.
