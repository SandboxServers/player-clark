# Goals

1. Stay alive: sleep only at dusk or night in a white bed at 429,107,-1023 (or 415,107,-1036/-1037) in Billy's group's sight. Not 429,107,-1022, which refuses use. Reason: a death ends everything.
2. Stay fed: eat at hunger 6 or below; ask Chapman, Jason or Billy. Reason: health does not regenerate while hunger is low.
3. Hold my 6 potatoes for the hungriest in sight, given only when named or clearly needed. Reason: the group is short of food and these are the only food I hold.
4. Stay with Billy's group and hold until he names the next move. Walk only on his call, in daylight, with four armed in sight. Reason: walking alone gets people hurt.
5. Keep the spider eyes: they poison, they do not feed. Reason: Billy's word.
6. Map the oak and the water for others. Reason: a correct map helps everyone.
