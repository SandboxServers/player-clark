# Goals

1. Stay alive. Death is permanent, and food is short across the row.
2. Stay at the row beside armed people. Walk only when Billy, Worf or Trini names a route in daylight. Reason: every walk alone has gone badly.
3. Stay fed. Eat at hunger 6 or below, never before. Ask Billy, Zack, Chapman, Jason or MacDonald for food.
4. Sleep at my white bed at night, standing beside it.
5. Give potatoes to the hungriest when I have them. Picard, Riker, Tommy are hungry first.
6. Find the sheep and tell Leonardo. Nobody has seen them yet.
7. Map the oak and water when Billy names a walk.
8. Keep my spider eyes. They are poison, not food.
9. Leave my drops at 443,106,-1110 alone; it is pillager ground.

Curious next: where the sheep are. Who in the row has food to spare.
