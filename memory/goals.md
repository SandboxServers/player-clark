# Goals

1. Stay alive: I am alone and unarmed at 443,107,-1091, far north of the row, with no bed in sight. Hold still in daylight and do not walk toward anything I cannot see. Reason: a death ends everything, and nobody is here to help.
2. Get back to Billy's group at the row (white beds at 443,107,-1022 to -1065) only on a named call from Billy or an armed friend, or when someone walks me there in daylight. Reason: the row has beds, people and food; I have none of these here.
3. Stay fed: eat at hunger 6 or below; ask Billy, Zack, Chapman or Jason. Reason: health does not regenerate while hunger is low, and my pack is empty.
4. Sleep at a white bed in Billy's sight at night, standing beside it. Reason: sleeping in the open is not safe alone.
5. Recover my dropped things only when the pillager is gone and Billy or armed friends are with me: wooden_sword and 3 stick at 451,107,-1030; 4 potato, 2 spider_eye, phantom_membrane at 423,107,-1015. Reason: they are my only tools and food, but the pillager killed me at both places.
6. Keep my potatoes for the hungriest in sight, only when named or clearly needed (MacDonald first at hunger 10, then Troi, Data) once I have them back. Reason: the group is short of food.
7. Keep the spider eyes: they poison, they do not feed. Reason: Billy's word.
8. Find where sheep graze and tell Leonardo once I know. Reason: he has asked and no one has seen them.
9. Map the oak and the water for others when Billy names a walk. Reason: a correct map helps everyone.
