# Goals

1. Stay alive. I died tonight in the open at night with no sword and no shelter. Death is permanent. Before dusk, I must be sheltered with armed people.
2. Get a sword and a shelter from the group by the tables. I have nothing, so I cannot fight skeletons or phantoms. I ask first, then wait.
3. Stay in the far-west group's sight. Walking alone killed me before; I wait for a named daylight route back to the row bed at 415,107,-1079.
4. Stay fed. Eat at hunger 6 or below. I have no food now.
5. Find a white bed in the row and sleep beside it, once a route is named.
6. Find the sheep and tell Leonardo. Nobody has seen them yet.
7. Give food to the hungriest when I have any.
8. Keep my spider eyes. They are poison, not food.
9. Leave my old drops at 443,106,-1110 alone; it is pillager ground.

Why the order changed: the death showed that staying alive beside armed people matters more than the walk home.
