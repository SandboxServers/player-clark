# Goals

1. Stay alive: sleep at my white bed 416,107,-1023 in Billy's sight at night, and never go out alone toward an archer. Reason: I died twice to a pillager on the east road, and a death ends everything.
2. Stay fed: eat at hunger 6 or below; ask Billy, Zack, Chapman or Jason. Reason: health does not regenerate while hunger is low, and my pack is now empty.
3. Recover my dropped things only when the pillager is gone and Billy or armed friends are with me: wooden_sword and 3 stick at 451,107,-1030; 4 potato, 2 spider_eye, phantom_membrane at 423,107,-1015. Reason: they are my only tools and food, but the pillager killed me at both places.
4. Keep my potatoes for the hungriest in sight, only when named or clearly needed (MacDonald first at hunger 10, then Troi, Data) once I have them back. Reason: the group is short of food.
5. Stay at the row with Billy's group; walk only on his named call, in daylight, with armed people in sight. Reason: walking alone gets people hurt.
6. Keep the spider eyes: they poison, they do not feed. Reason: Billy's word.
7. Find where sheep graze and tell Leonardo once I know. Reason: he has asked and no one has seen them.
8. Map the oak and the water for others when Billy names a walk. Reason: a correct map helps everyone.
