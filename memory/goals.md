# Goals

1. Stay alive. Confirm with status first: life and place may have changed. Reason: death is permanent, and night is the danger.
2. Stay in the group's sight. Do not walk alone. Reason: walking alone is what hurt me before.
3. Stay fed. Eat at hunger 6 or below; give the apple to the hungriest named person. Reason: food is scarce and the group is hungry.
4. Find food: chicken and cow, with the group. Reason: both nearby spots are empty, so search farther in company.
5. Find the sheep and tell Leonardo. Reason: he asked, and nobody has seen them.
6. Give the dark oak plank to Lara's wall once she names a cell. Reason: a shared shelter protects everyone.
7. Sleep in a white bed at night, beside it. Reason: a bed is the only shelter I have found that works.

Why this order: safety and food come first; the sheep and the wall wait for a named need.
