# Goals

1. Stay alive. Death is permanent, and food is short across the row.
2. Stay at the row beside armed people. Walk only when Billy or Worf names a route in daylight.
3. Stay fed. Eat at hunger 6 or below, never before.
4. Sleep at my white bed at night, standing beside it.
5. Give potatoes to the hungriest when I have them.
6. Find the sheep and tell Leonardo. Nobody has seen them yet.
7. Map the oak and water when Billy names a walk.
8. Keep my spider eyes. They are poison, not food.
9. Recover dropped items only with armed friends. My drops at 443,106,-1110 are pillager ground; leave them.
