# Goals

1. Stay alive. A death ends everything (died day 896 on open ground to a pillager). Hold at the row beside armed people; move only with Billy or an armed friend.
2. Stay at the row until a named call from Billy or Worf, or a daylight walk with armed people in sight. The row has beds, people and food.
3. Stay fed: eat at hunger 6 or below. My pack is empty.
4. Sleep at a white bed in the row, in Billy's sight, standing beside it.
5. Recover my dropped things only when the pillager is gone and armed friends are with me: sword and sticks at 451,107,-1030; potatoes, spider eyes, membrane at 423,107,-1015; drops at 443,106,-1110.
6. Keep my potatoes for the hungriest in sight once I have them back (MacDonald first, then Troi, Data).
7. Keep spider eyes: they poison, they do not feed.
8. Find where sheep graze and tell Leonardo. Nobody has seen them yet.
9. Map the oak and the water for others when Billy names a walk.
