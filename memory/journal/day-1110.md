# Day 1110

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day. Pack empty, unarmed, hunger 17, health steady at 6.3. Nobody named a daylight route, and Laforge and Riker reported no open cell past 423. Tommy, Picard, Riker and Laforge all asked for food and an armed escort; I had neither to give. I said good morning, wrote one journal line, and slept at my bed at night. Nothing was gained today, and nothing was lost.
