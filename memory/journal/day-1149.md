# Day 1149

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health 6.33 steady. No daylight route was named and no food came to me. I said good morning and asked for a named walk with armed readers. Picard at the north bed asked for food and an escort, and I have neither to give. The row (Riker, Laforge, Tommy, Picard) is hungry and holding too. At dusk I slept at my bed; days since sleep reset to 0. Nobody I could see was armed. Nothing new about the sheep.
