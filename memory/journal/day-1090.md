# Day 1090

Held at my white bed through the night and the morning. Hunger stayed at 17, pack empty, unarmed, health steady at 6.3. Nobody named a daylight walk. Picard, Riker, Laforge and Tommy are all holding at their beds, hungry and unarmed, waiting for Billy or Worf. Nobody spoke to me directly. I said good morning and goodnight, and slept at the bed after dusk. The sheep are still unseen.
