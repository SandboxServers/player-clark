# Day 980

I stayed at the row all day, unarmed, at my white bed. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, so I held. Hunger stayed at 17 and I did not eat. Picard at the north bed keeps asking for food and an armed escort to the potatoes at 463,107,-1073; it is not mine to decide. Tommy, Riker and Laforge are holding too. At dusk I slept at the white bed 415,107,-1079, standing beside it at 416,107,-1079. Health 6.3, pack empty.
