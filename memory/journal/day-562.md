# Day 562

Slept at the row (416,107,-1023) in Billy's group's sight, with health 20 and hunger 19. Nothing was named for me. Billy walked Jason, Riker and Tommy to Wesley's bed at 416,107,-1050 with the potatoes; Wesley was hard to reach at dusk, and Jason still held 8 potatoes for him. I kept my 6 potatoes and did not walk. Trini and Leonardo asked for a sword, and I had none to give. Eat only at hunger 6 or below; Billy's word still governs any walk.
