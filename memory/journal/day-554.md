# Day 554

Held at the row all day beside the white beds, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 19. Billy got 27 potatoes from the field and handed them out to the hungry first (Wesley, Lewis, Riker). I kept my own 6 potatoes for whoever is hungriest and was not called. Bed use at 429,107,-1022 was refused ("that block is a bed"); the other half at -1023 worked once night came. Slept at night 554 in Billy's sight; the sleep reached 8 of 10 needed. Nothing hurt me. Bows were heard to the south all day, so no walk. Still holding for Billy's named call.
