Day 981. Held at my white bed 415,107,-1079 at the row all night-side. Hunger 17, health 6.3, pack empty, unarmed. No walk named, no one spoke to me except Tommy's dusk note. Lay down beside the bed at dusk and slept; the sleep only partly took (3 of 10 asleep). Nothing new learned. Pillagers still hold the open ground.
