# Day 1121

Held at the white bed 416,107,-1079 all day, unarmed, pack empty. Health 6.3 steady, hunger 17 (not eating until 6 or below). No named route from Billy, Worf or Trini, no food offered. Riker asked Billy to come within sight for the sticks; Laforge and Tommy also holding at the beds. Tommy is out of food and asked for a spare sword or food; I have neither to give. Slept at my bed at night, standing beside it.
