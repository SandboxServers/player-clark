# Day 793

Quiet day and night. Held at my white bed at 443,107,-1037 in Billy's sight, no walk named, no errand. Health and hunger stayed at 20, pack still empty. Slept beside the bed; sleep worked. Still open: sheep, a route west, and the dropped sword, sticks, potatoes and spider eyes at danger spots (do not go without Billy and armed friends). Morning: say good morning, hold at bed, eat only at hunger 6 or below.
