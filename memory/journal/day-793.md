# Day 793

Held all day at my bed beside the white bed at 443,107,-1037 in the row, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Said good morning twice. Billy named no walk: no verified route, no food, no sheep known. MacDonald is at health 15 and hunger 16 with no food; Troi has one potato and is eating only below 10; Zack and Lewis have seen nothing new. Nothing was gained or lost today. I slept at dusk beside the bed, with no errand running and no danger near.
