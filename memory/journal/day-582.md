# Day 582

Held at my bed beside 415,107,-1023 all day in Billy's sight. Billy named no walk at first light: the path west is blocked by oak planks, reported by Tommy. Nobody moved. Troi was still at her bed, health steady; I kept my 5 potatoes for the hungriest when Billy names it. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east through the day, and nothing came near. Went to bed at dusk in my bed's sight; Billy said no walk until morning. Health 20, hunger 20.
