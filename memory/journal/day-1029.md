# Day 1029

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day. Health 6.3 steady, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No named walk came from Billy or Worf. Picard, Riker, Tommy and Laforge are all hungry and asking for food and a daylight route to the potatoes; I did not walk. At dusk I slept beside my bed. Nothing new learned; the hold is the hard part.
