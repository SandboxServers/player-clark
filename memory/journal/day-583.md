# Day 583

Held at my bed 415,107,-1023 all day in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, no damage. Billy named no walk: planks still block the west route to the row's far beds. Bows were drawn to the east and southeast now and then, but nobody was hurt. Heard Riker owes a debt of 4 cobblestone that I cannot pay yet. Slept at dusk in my bed with the group holding. Troi and Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) remain the ones to watch for food. My 5 potatoes are still held for the hungriest when Billy names it.
