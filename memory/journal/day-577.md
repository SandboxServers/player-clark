# Day 577

Held at the row in Billy's sight all day. Health 20, hunger 20 the whole time. Billy named no walk, so I did not walk. Troi (health 8, hunger 17) is the one to watch; I told him my 5 potatoes are his when Billy names it. Lewis, Worf, Jason, Wesley, Zack and MacDonald all held at the row waiting for Billy. Jason's beds were blocked more than once on the way to them, so walking stayed off the list. Bows were drawn to the southeast twice. At dusk I went to my bed at 415,107,-1023 and slept in the row's sight.
