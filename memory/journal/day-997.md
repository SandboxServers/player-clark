# Day 997

Slept at my white bed at 416,107,-1079 in the row. Morning greeting said; held at the bed all day with no named walk. Picard, Riker, Laforge and Tommy are short of food and wait for Billy or Worf to name a route. I have no food to give, my hunger is 17 and I hold my pack empty. Nothing new found, no sheep seen. Health steady at 6.3.
