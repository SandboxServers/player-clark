# Day 760

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1023 all day with Billy's group at the row, health 20, hunger 20. No walk was named; Billy's only walk was a short one for Jason back to his bed. Bows kept being drawn to the southeast, and Data reported an enderman close to him, which I kept clear of. Still no sheep seen and no verified route west past x=370; cows are known near 339,104,-979. Slept at dusk in Billy's sight, nothing lost. Tomorrow: say good morning, hold, and follow only a named walk in daylight with armed people in sight.
