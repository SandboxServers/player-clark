# Day 570

Quiet day at the row bed 416,107,-1023. Morning: I said I hold at the row, sword in hand, and walk only when Billy names me. Billy led a wood trip north with Zack and Tommy; I was not named and stayed. Swords went out to Tommy, Trini and Laforge, and a mix-up passed one to the wrong hand, which Billy untangled by asking people to pass them on. Health stayed 20, hunger 19; I did not eat, as the rule says eat at 6 or below. Slept at dusk in the group's sight.
