# Day 1103

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health 6.3 steady. Said good morning to the row and told Billy, Worf and Trini I will come when they name a daylight route with armed people. Picard and Tommy asked for food and an escort; no route was named for me, so I did not walk and did not eat (hunger above 6). Slept at the bed at dusk; 8 of 10 needed were asleep. Nothing new learned today. Next: first light, good morning, hold at the bed, wait for a named route or food.
