# Day 1056

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day. Health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No named daylight walk came from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Picard, Tommy, Laforge and Riker all held at their beds too, short of food. Picard asked for food and an escort to the potatoes; I had none to give. Slept at night at my bed; the night passed.
