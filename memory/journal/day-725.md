# Day 725

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 all day in Billy's row. No walk was named; Billy has still seen no verified route past x=370, and nobody has seen sheep. Wesley's walk to bed 429,107,-1036 was blocked again, and he is holding at 418,107,-1047; I still have not given him the wooden sword, since Billy has not said he is near. Bows were heard to the southeast and east all day, source unseen. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Slept at dusk in the row with 7 of 10 asleep; nothing hurt me. Nothing new learned about the land today.
