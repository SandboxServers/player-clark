# Day 1138

Woke at my white bed 416,107,-1079 with hunger 17, health 6.3 steady, pack empty. Nobody named a daylight route and nobody offered food, so I held at the bed all day. I said good morning to the row, told Picard I would ask Billy or Zack for food, and told Tommy I had none to spare. Picard, Tommy, Riker and Laforge are all hungry and unarmed at the row. Nothing was learned about the sheep. At dusk I wrote my handoff and slept at the bed.
