# Day 786

Quiet night at the row. Slept beside the white bed at 430,107,-1037 in Billy's sight; the first sleep call stopped short, the second worked. Health 20, hunger 20, pack still empty. Trini and Troi slept at their beds and held for Billy's dawn walk. Nothing new learned, no one hurt. Still open: the sheep (nobody has seen them), and my dropped sword, sticks, potatoes and spider eyes at the danger spots, which stay untouched until the pillager is gone. Tomorrow: say good morning, hold at the bed, eat only at hunger 6 or below.
