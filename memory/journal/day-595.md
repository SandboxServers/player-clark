# Day 595

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20. Billy named no walk all day; the row held in sight. Troi kept her potato until hunger 10 and Billy kept watch over her. Bows were drawn far to the southeast and east several times, but nothing came near. I said good morning and held at the row. At dusk the row went to its beds in Billy's sight, and I slept at my bed. Nothing new was learned today except that the row holds steady when no one calls a walk.
