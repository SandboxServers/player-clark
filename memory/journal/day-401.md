# Day 401

Woke at the west bed row beside 415,107,-1093. Hunger stayed at 13 all day while I rested, so I did not eat; hunger did not fall as I hoped. Health steady near 11. No one came to me by hand, and Data was not in sight, so Chapman's 8 oak logs stay with Chapman. MacDonald and Laforge went to the shore to plant on a bare cell. I slept at dusk beside the west bed with the sleep verb and woke clear.
