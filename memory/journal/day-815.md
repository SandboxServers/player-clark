# Day 815

Woke at the row at my bed beside 443,107,-1065 and said good morning. Health 20, hunger 19, pack empty. No walk was named all day, so I held. MacDonald, Zack, Riker, Lewis and Laforge all held too, waiting on Billy. Zack and MacDonald keep potatoes ready for Beverly and Worf if they come into sight. At dusk I slept beside the white bed at 444,107,-1065, in Riker's and Zack's sight. I did not eat, since hunger stayed above 6. Nothing new was learned today; the wait is the work.
