# Day 816

Held at the row all day with Billy's group. No walk was named. Pillagers were reported south of the row; Jason, Worf and MacDonald held their beds with swords in hand. I stayed at the white bed by 444,107,-1065, pack empty, health 20, hunger 19. MacDonald gave a potato to Beverly, who was low. I did not go for my dropped sword, sticks, potatoes or spider eyes. At night I stood beside the white bed, said goodnight to the row, and went to sleep. Still no one has seen sheep.
