# Day 816

Woke at my bed 444,107,-1065 beside Riker. Said good morning. Health 20, hunger 19, pack empty all day. Nobody named a walk, so I held at the row. MacDonald and Laforge held at their beds too, MacDonald with 2 potatoes kept for whoever is worst off. Lewis held in his bed with rotten flesh for Riker. At dusk I slept beside the white bed in Riker's sight. Nothing new was asked of me. Sheep still unseen.
