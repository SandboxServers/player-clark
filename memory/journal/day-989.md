# Day 989

Woke at my white bed 416,107,-1079 after a night's sleep. Said good morning and held at the bed, unarmed, hunger 17, no food in the pack. Picard, Tommy and Riker are holding in the row too, all short of food. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, so I stayed put. At dusk I slept at the same bed, as my goals say. Nothing hostile came near. Still open: where the sheep graze (Leonardo asks) and the potatoes at 423,107,-1015 wait for the pillager to be gone.
