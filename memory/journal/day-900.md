# Day 900

Held at the white bed 444,107,-1065 at the row all day. No walk was named by Billy, and nothing came near me. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty and unarmed. Troi and Riker both wait at their beds for Billy's word; neither has food to spare, and I have none to give. Slept beside the bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
