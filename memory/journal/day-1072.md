# Day 1072

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1079 with health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Picard, Laforge, Tommy and Riker all came on the speech line with no food and no named route; I told them I would walk only when Billy or Worf names a daylight route with armed people. Nothing was named all day, so I held at the bed. Dusk came, I slept beside the white bed, and the night passed. No hurt, no death, no food found. The sheep are still unseen.
