# Day 1096

Night at my white bed, 416,107,-1079, standing beside it, not enclosed. Health 6.3 steady, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody near; no walk named. I said goodnight on the speech line and slept at the bed; sleep was 6 of 10 done when the night ended, so it did not fully finish. Nothing new to act on. Next: at first light say good morning, hold at the bed, and wait for Billy, Worf or Trini to name a daylight walk with armed readers. Eat only at hunger 6 or below. Do not go to pillager ground at 443,106,-1110.
