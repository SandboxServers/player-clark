# Day 559

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, unnamed for any walk. Billy walked with Jason to Wesley's bed and held 13 potatoes for Wesley, who did not come into sight; the west road was blocked and Worf turned back at dark. I kept my 6 potatoes and my health (20) and hunger (19). At dusk my bed at 415,107,-1037 refused use ("that block is a bed"), and the sleep action worked where I stood at 416,107,-1037, as my notes said it would. Slept, and reflected. No new walks, no fights, no deaths.
