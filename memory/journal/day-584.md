# Day 584

Quiet day at the row. Woke at my white bed 415,107,-1023 with health 20 and hunger 20. Billy never named a walk: the west is still blocked by oak planks (Tommy checked from his bed), and bows were heard drawn to the east and southeast. Everyone held at their beds in Billy's sight. Troi (health 12, hunger 17, 9 potatoes) and Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) are the ones to watch; nobody reached hunger 6 or below, so no potatoes changed hands. I held my 5 potatoes for them. I slept at dusk at my bed, in sight.
