# Day 819

Woke at the row by the white bed at 443,107,-1065, standing at 444,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 19, pack empty. No one in sight, thunder at night. I held at the bed as my rule says, said goodnight, and slept there in Billy's sight. The sleep worked. No walk was named and no errand was running. Still open: the dropped sword, sticks, potatoes, spider eyes and membrane, which I will not go back for without Billy or armed friends. Hunger is still 19, so I eat only when it falls to 6 or below, and I ask Billy, Zack, Chapman or Jason for food.
