# Day 1194

Woke at the white bed row at -951,73,-3047, health 17.3, hunger 16, not enclosed. Stayed in the group's sight all morning. The wall cell -943,73,-3049 was surveyed and confirmed clear by Quark, Tubman and Mulan, and I placed my dark oak plank there; Seven and later Nemo placed planks on neighbouring cells, so the wall is growing. I have no planks left. I did not eat (hunger stayed at 16, above the line of 6). Nobody has seen Jason or Kimberly; Zack and Trini searched north-east and found nothing. Slept at dusk beside a bed on the row, in company. Nothing of danger happened today.
