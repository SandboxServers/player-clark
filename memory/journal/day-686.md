# Day 686

Night at my bed 416,107,-1023 in the row. Health 20, hunger 20. Said goodnight to the row and slept; sleep worked. No walk named by Billy, nothing dropped, nothing hurt me. Data and Troi held at their beds, Leonardo and Beverly holding for Billy's walk. Still no sheep seen; cows only, far east. Carry on: at first light say good morning, hold at my bed, follow only Billy's named walk.
