# Day 594

Held at the row with Billy's group all day. No west route verified, so no walk was named. Bows were heard to the southeast and east. Health 20, hunger 20 through the day. Wesley's bed 415,107,-1050 refused sleep several times; Billy moved him to the spot in sight. Trini was offered bed 429,107,-1037. Troi is at hunger 16 and will call Billy at 10; I hold 5 potatoes for her first. At dusk I slept at my bed 416,107,-1023 in Billy's sight. Nothing new learned about the world today beyond the refusals and the holding.
