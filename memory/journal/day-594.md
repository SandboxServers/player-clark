# Day 594

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 in Billy's sight all day. No walk was named; the row still has no verified west route. Trini and Wesley tried beds by day and were told to hold until dusk. Bows were heard to the east and southeast. At dusk I slept at my bed with health 20 and hunger 20. Potatoes (5) are still held for Troi first when Billy names it. Billy, Jason, Chapman, Lewis, Zack and the others all held in sight.
