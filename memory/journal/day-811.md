# Day 811

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day. Said good morning. No walk was named, so nothing was gathered or walked. Health stayed at 20 and hunger at 20, with my pack empty. Riker (hunger 17) and Zack (holding two potatoes for Worf) are waiting on Billy too. Laforge and Lewis are unarmed and holding. At dusk I slept beside the white bed at 443,107,-1065 in the row's sight. My sword, sticks, potatoes, spider eyes and membrane are still at the danger spots, and I did not go back for them.
