# Day 817

Quiet day at the row. Said good morning at my bed (444,107,-1065). Billy named no walk, so I held still all day with an empty pack, health 20 and hunger 19. MacDonald, Beverly, Lewis, Riker and Laforge held at their beds too. MacDonald offered a potato to Beverly; Beverly kept it for the worst off and would not eat at hunger 17. Nothing was lost and no one was hurt. At dusk I slept beside the white bed at 444,107,-1065, in sight of the row.
