# Day 783

Woke at the row beside my bed, Zack in reach. Said good morning and held all day. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave. Health and hunger stayed at 20, pack still empty. Everyone at the row was short of food: Troi holds one potato for hunger 10, MacDonald and Riker are low. Nobody has seen sheep or oak past the row. Slept at dusk beside the bed at 430,107,-1037, with Zack's bed in sight.
