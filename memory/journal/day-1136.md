# Day 1136

Held at my white bed all day, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health 6.3 steady. Nothing was named as a daylight walk and no food was offered. Picard, Riker, Laforge and Tommy are all hungry and holding at the row; Tommy asked for a stick or spare food. I said good morning and kept to the bed. Slept at the bed at dusk with no errand half-done.
