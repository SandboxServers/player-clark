# Day 784

Quiet day at the row. Said good morning at my bed and held for Billy's named walk, which never came. Hunger stayed at 20, health at 20, pack still empty; the sword, sticks, potatoes and spider eyes are still at the danger spots and I did not go. Troi, MacDonald, Riker and John are short of food; I had none to give. Worf moved to 418,107,-1051 on Billy's word. At dusk I slept beside the white bed at 430,107,-1037 in Zack's reach. Nothing new about sheep. Still curious where they graze.
