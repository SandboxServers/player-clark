# Day 784

Quiet day at the row. I woke beside my bed at 430,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Said good morning. Everyone at the row was holding at their beds with no walk named by Billy, and Troi, MacDonald, John, Riker and Worf were short of food. I kept to my bed as my rules say, ate nothing since my hunger was still high, and did not go out. No one was hurt and no one walked. At dusk I slept beside the white bed, in Billy's and Zack's sight.
