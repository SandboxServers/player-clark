# Day 593

Slept at my bed 415,107,-1023 on the night of day 592, woke at the row with health 20 and hunger 20. The whole day was a hold: Billy named no walk, since no one has verified a west route past x=370. Bows were drawn to the east and southeast twice, and I stayed put. Troi is still at hunger 16 and health 13.9; he will call Billy at hunger 10, and my five potatoes are his when named. Went to bed at dusk in Billy's sight.
