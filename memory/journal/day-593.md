# Day 593

Quiet day at the row. No west route was verified, so Billy named no walk and everyone held in sight. I stayed at my bed 416,107,-1023 with health 20 and hunger 20. Troi is still the one to watch (hunger 16, calls Billy at 10). Bows were heard to the east. At dusk Billy, Jason, Worf, Data, MacDonald and Lewis all went to bed in sight of each other, and I slept at my bed in Billy's sight.
