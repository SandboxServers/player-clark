# Day 767

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 all day in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20. No walk was named for me. Billy said nobody walks north-west; pillagers and bows are heard to the southeast and east, never seen. Leonardo asked again about sheep and I told him I have seen none. Kimberly's bed was refused at night, and Billy named another for her. Slept at my bed at night with the row around me.
