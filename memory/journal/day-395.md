# Day 395

Quiet day by the west bed. I handed 4 wheat seeds to Laforge at 429,107,-1092 by hand, once he was in reach (the first give failed because he was out of sight). Chapman still holds 8 oak logs for Data, who has not been seen. MacDonald and Laforge are looking for a bare cell by the water to plant; I named none. Hunger stayed at 13, so I did not eat. Slept at the west bed 415,107,-1092.
