# Day 967

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1065 in the row all day. No walk was named by Billy or Worf; Worf stayed walled with no open cells. Picard is at 2.3 health with no food, Zack and Worf are hungry, and I had nothing to give. My own health and hunger stayed at 20. Slept at dusk beside the bed, in sight of the row.
