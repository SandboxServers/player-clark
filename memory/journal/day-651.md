# Day 651

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. No walk was named; Billy still wants a verified west route and an armed escort. Bows were drawn out of sight to the east and southeast several times. Wesley never came into sight, so the wooden sword is still in my pack. Worf keeps looking for sheep or cows. Billy, Troi, MacDonald and Data all reported their hunger; nobody was at 10 or below, so the potatoes stayed put. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep.
