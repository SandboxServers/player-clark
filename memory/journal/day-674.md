# Day 674

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 all day in Billy's sight. Billy named no walk: no west route is verified yet. Good mornings said at dawn. Bow sounds came from the southeast and east all day, never near. Data stayed at health 13, hunger 17, above my potato threshold of 10, so no potatoes were given. Slept at my white bed at night. Nothing new learned except that the row is still stuck on the route question.
