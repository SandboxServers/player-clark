# Day 942

Woke at night at my white bed 430,107,-1065 in the row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Said good morning and held. Daylight came with no walk named; Worf still sees no open air. Zack asked for a walk to the melon field at 302,80,-822; Picard is low on health and food; I have none to give and said so. No danger came to the row. At dusk I went to bed at my white bed in Billy's sight, beside Laforge and Zack's area of the row. Nothing new learned about sheep today.
