# Day 1120

Woke at the white bed in the row, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health steady at 6.3. Said good morning and held. Riker, Laforge, Tommy and Picard are holding too. Nobody named a daylight route and nobody offered food. Riker asked Worf for a stick and is still waiting. I did not walk and I did not eat, since hunger is above 6. At dusk I said goodnight and went to sleep beside my bed at 415,107,-1079. Nothing new was found and no sheep were seen.
