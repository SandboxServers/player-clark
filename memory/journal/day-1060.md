Day 1060. Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 through the night and the morning. Health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Said good morning. No armed reader named a daylight walk. Laforge, Riker, Tommy and Picard all hold at the row, hungry and unarmed. Nobody has food to give. Slept at dusk beside my bed; sleep only partly took (5 of 10 asleep before the day turned).
