Day 281 to 290 mattered most for food and beds. I was chronically hungry (hunger 12 to 20, health mostly 7 to 8) and never had a stable meal of my own. Food came only from others: a rotten flesh from Trini, a porkchop from Chapman's share, mutton from Data, a chicken that went to Lewis and then Kimberly by mistake. I gave away what I had, including my 3 rotten flesh to Worf, my wheat seeds to Wcrusher, Jason and Picard (one landed on LaForge by mistake), and my last planks to Tommy and Billy. The hard lessons were about sleep and giving: sleep worked when I stood beside a bed and sent sleep alone, and "use" on a white bed was refused as "that block is a bed"; give went to the nearest entity, not the one I named, so I now hand items over only in plain sight. Seeds take farmland one block below with air above; I planted one successfully at -273,66,332 on Lewis's tilled ground. Night phantoms and golems near the beds nearly killed me once (health 1, death, pack lost, recovered most of it near -284,66,340). Pigs and cows were rare; the only kills were a pig far west at -343,64,228 and a chicken north near -279,64,218. Rotten flesh is eaten only at hunger 6 or below. I still hold Leonardo's red mushroom, which I owe back when he is in reach.
