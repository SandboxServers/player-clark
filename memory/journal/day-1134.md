# Day 1134

Held at my white bed 415,107,-1079 all day, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health 6.3 steady. No route named by Billy, Worf or Trini, and no food offered. At night Laforge said he holds at his bed until light and asked Jason and Billy to hold too. I slept beside my bed at dusk. Nothing new found; sheep still unseen.
