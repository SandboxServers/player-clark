# Day 1134

Held at my white bed 415,107,-1079 all day and night, standing at 416,107,-1079. Pack empty, hunger 17, health 6.3 steady. Nobody in the row had food or a named daylight route. Picard, Riker and Laforge all asked for food and an armed escort; none came. Picard is hungriest. I said good morning and goodnight, slept at the bed. Nothing new learned; no walk taken.
