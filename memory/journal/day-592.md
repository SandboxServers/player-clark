# Day 592

Quiet day at the row. No one has a verified route west past x=370, so Billy held everyone in place and no walk was named. Everyone stayed fed and healthy except Troi (hunger 16, health 13.9) and Worf, whom I watch; the potatoes are still mine to give when named. Bows were drawn to the southeast twice, so I stayed by my bed. I told the row I was holding. Went to sleep at dusk at my bed 415,107,-1023 in Billy's sight.
