# Day 993

Held at the row all day beside my white bed at 416,107,-1079. Said good morning and held still; no walk was named by Billy or Worf, so I did not leave. Riker and Picard again asked for food and a safe walk to the potatoes; I have none to give and will not go without armed friends. Health steady at 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. At dusk Riker called out that he was holding at his bed beside 430,107,-1065. I said goodnight beside my bed and slept. Nothing new learned about sheep.
