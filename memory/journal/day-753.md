# Day 753

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 all day and through the night. Health 20, hunger 20, no harm. No walk was named by Billy, so nobody went out. The row talked of sheep again; still none seen, and Leonardo is waiting on Billy's word. MacDonald is at hunger 16 and Data at 17, both above the mark of 10 for handing out potatoes, so my five potatoes stayed in my pack. Data has an enderman close by; I kept my eyes off it. Bows were heard to the east and southeast all day. I slept at the bed in the night and woke at the start of day 754.
