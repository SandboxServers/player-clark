# Day 309

Woke at dawn at the north white beds with full health and hunger. Troi and Kimberly were the lowest in reach; others (Bcrusher, Data, Jason) fed them, and I kept my 3 raw chicken for whoever dropped to hunger 6 or below. Nobody did, so I kept it. The group stayed at the beds through the day. Sleep failed in daylight ("not night"), then worked at night beside the north white bed: first 3 of 10 asleep, then fully. A far iron golem was seen to the south-east but did not come near. Lesson: sleep only works at night, and it can take two tries. Still to do: hunt for meat with the group, and return the red mushroom owed to Leonardo.
