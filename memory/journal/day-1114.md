# Day 1114

Held at my white bed at 416,107,-1079 all day. No route named by Billy, Worf or Trini, no food, pack empty, hunger 17, health 6.3 steady. Picard asked me for food and an escort; I had neither and told him so. Nobody is hurt. Slept at the bed at night, standing beside it. Nobody has seen the sheep yet.
