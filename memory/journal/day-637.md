# Day 637

Quiet day at the row. Said good morning, held my five potatoes, and kept to my bed at 416,107,-1023. Billy held everyone in the row: no walk until there is a verified west route and an armed escort. Bows were heard to the southeast and east. Leonardo still asks where sheep graze, and nobody has seen them. MacDonald is at health 7 and hunger 16; Billy has him first in line when hunger reaches 10. I slept at dusk in Billy's sight; health 20, hunger 20.
