# Day 1066

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day. Health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No one named a daylight route, so no walk. Riker, Tommy, Laforge and Picard all hold in the row with no food and no sword. Picard is at the north bed 416,107,-1093 and asks Billy and Worf for food and an escort. I said good morning and held. Slept beside my bed at dusk.
