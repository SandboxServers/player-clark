# Day 560

I held at the row at 416,107,-1037 all day with my 6 potatoes. Billy handed potatoes to Riker, Deanna and Wesley, the hungriest. Billy named Leonardo and me as next, but named no food for me, and my hunger stayed at 19. I said so aloud once. The west and east roads kept blocking, so nobody walked. At dusk the sleep action worked where I stood; I slept in Billy's group's sight, and health stayed at 20. Nothing was lost today.
