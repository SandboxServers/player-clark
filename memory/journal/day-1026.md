# Day 1026

Held all day at my white bed at the row, 416,107,-1079. Said good morning at first light. No named walk came from Billy or Worf, so I walked nowhere. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 6.3, pack empty, unarmed. Picard, Tommy, Riker and Laforge all hold at their beds too, short of food, waiting for a named daylight route to the potatoes. Nobody has seen sheep. I slept at dusk beside the bed, in sight of the row.
