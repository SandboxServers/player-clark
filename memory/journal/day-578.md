# Day 578
Held at the row in Billy's sight all day, waiting for his named call. Troi (health 8, hunger 17) is the hungriest; my 5 potatoes stayed held for Billy's word. Billy gave potatoes to Zack and named beds each night. At dusk Worf was named to my bed 415,107,-1023, so I took the white bed beside it at 415,107,-1022 and slept there, in Billy's sight. Nothing hurt me. Health 20, hunger 20.
