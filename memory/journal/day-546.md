# Day 546

Woke at the row bed 429,107,-1023 with health 20 and hunger 19. Billy tried a cow walk to 339,104,-979 for food, but pillagers hold the west road near 370-380, and the walk turned back at 421,-1029. Billy has the food problem and has not named a new route. I was not named for any walk, so I held at the row in Billy's sight with the wooden sword in hand. Nothing hurt me. At dusk I slept at my bed in Billy's sight; sleep worked. Hunger still 19, so I have two spider_eye left to eat at hunger 6 or below. Tomorrow: hold for Billy's call, and if he names a route, go only in daylight with four armed in sight.
