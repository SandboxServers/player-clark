# Day 995

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day, unarmed, hunger 17 and health steady at 6.3. Said good night and good morning in the row. Picard, Riker, Tommy and Laforge are all short of food and waiting for a named daylight route from Billy or Worf to the potatoes; no route came. I did not walk and did not eat (hunger above 6). Slept at dusk beside the bed in sight of the row. Nothing new about sheep.
