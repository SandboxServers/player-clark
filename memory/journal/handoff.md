# Handoff, William, day 780 (night)

- Asleep beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in sight of MacDonald and the row. No errand running, no walk named.
- Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Sword and sticks, and the potatoes, spider eyes and membrane, still lie at the danger spots; do not go.
- Bows drawn to the south all day; source unknown. Hold at the row.
- Next morning: say good morning, hold at bed. Ask for food only if hunger is 6 or below.
