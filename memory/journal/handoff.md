Handoff, night of day 1027.
William at my white bed 416,107,-1079 at the row. Health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No errand, no named walk. Not in danger.
Picard asked for food and an escort to the potatoes; I told him I hold until Billy or Worf names a walk.
Next waking: say good morning, hold, eat only at hunger 6 or below, walk only when Billy or Worf names it with armed people in sight.
