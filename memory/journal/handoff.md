# Handoff, day 1091 night

At white bed 416,107,-1079, standing beside it. Health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No errand running. Tommy holds at his bed, still hungry and wants food and a sword. Eat only at hunger 6 or below. Do not go to pillager ground at 443,106,-1110. Sheep still unseen. Next: at first light say good morning, hold at the bed, wait for Billy or Worf to name a daylight walk.
