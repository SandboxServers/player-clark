# Handoff, William, day 813 (slept)

- Pillagers with crossbows came in at the south-west of the row and shot me; I fled north-east to 445,107,-1059. Health back to 20.
- Slept beside white bed 443,107,-1064 (I stood at 444,107,-1064), in Riker's and Zack's sight.
- No errand running, no walk named. Billy holds the row; Jason and Worf hold at their beds with swords.
- Beverly is at health 1, has no things; MacDonald is holding a potato for her. My dropped sword and sticks (451,107,-1030) and potatoes, spider eyes, membrane (423,107,-1015) still lie where they were; do not go.
- Health 20, hunger 19, pack empty.
- Next morning: say good morning, hold at bed, eat only at hunger 6 or below, walk only on Billy's named call.
