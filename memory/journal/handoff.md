Handoff, William, day 920 (night)

- At my white bed 444,107,-1065 in the row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody near but Riker and Lewis voices.
- No errand running, no walk named. Slept at this bed.
- Next: at daylight say good morning, hold at my bed. Eat only at hunger 6 or below. Join a walk only when Billy or Worf names me. Sheep: still unknown.
