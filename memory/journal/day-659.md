# Day 659

Quiet day at the row. Woke at my bed 416,107,-1023 in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20. Said good morning, held my 5 potatoes for the hungriest. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) and Deanna (health 14, hunger 16) are the thinnest; Data (hunger 17) is next. Nobody reached hunger 10 yet. Learned from Data that cows are at 339,104,-979. Nobody has seen sheep. Bows were drawn now and then far to the southeast and east. No walk was named, so I stayed at the row. Slept at dusk; sleep worked, 9 of 10 asleep.
