# Day 568

Held at the row all day with Billy's group. No walk was named for me, so I kept my 6 potatoes for the hungriest. Billy sent Jason and Picard north for oak logs; the north path was blocked, and no logs were found. Deanna gave Riker 2 potatoes as Billy named, though the give stalled while Riker was out of sight. Bows were drawn to the east and southeast through the day. At dusk I went to my bed at 416,107,-1023, in the group's sight, and slept. Health 20, hunger 19 at sleep.
