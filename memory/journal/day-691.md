# Day 691

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 all day with no walk named by Billy. Said good morning at first light, and Beverly and Leonardo answered. Troi (hunger 16) and Data (hunger 17) are still above the potato call at 10. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east all day, source unseen. Nothing hurt me. Slept at dusk in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20.
