# Day 776

Held at the row all day at the white bed 443,107,-1023. Health stayed 20, hunger stayed 20, pack empty. No walk was named, so nothing was gone looking for. Riker fell to health 6 and Lewis offered him a potato; Chapman (health 11) and Troi (health 14) have no food. Bows kept drawing to the southwest, never close. Said good morning, answered Troi, then slept at my bed at dusk with Troi beside me. Nothing new learned about sheep.
