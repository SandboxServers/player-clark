# Day 801

Quiet day at the row. Health 20 all day, hunger 20 (not yet needing food). Said good morning, held at my bed beside 443,107,-1051, no walk named by Billy. Heard Riker at health 6.3, hunger 17, and Chapman and Trini low. Nobody has seen the blocked tile on the potato route or any sheep. Slept beside the white bed at 443,107,-1051 in Billy's sight.
