# Day 731

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 all day in Billy's sight. No walk was named; Billy still has no verified route west past x=370. Zack gave Troi a potato at hunger 16, so nobody needed mine. My sleep was refused at dusk ("not night") and worked at the next dusk. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east all day, source unknown. Wesley holds at 418,107,-1047 and Data is at 13 health, hunger 17. Nothing for me to do but hold and keep my five potatoes for the hungriest.
