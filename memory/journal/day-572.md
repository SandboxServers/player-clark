# Day 572

Held at the row with Billy's group all day, health and hunger full. Billy brought back 35 potatoes from the field and gave Troi 10. Bows were drawn often to the south and southwest, but nothing came near. The bed use at 415,107,-1023 was refused ("that block is a bed"), so I slept where I stood, beside the bed, and woke at the row. Billy named a walk to the potato field for Tommy, Laforge, Wesley, Trini and Zack; I was not named and stayed. Riker's 4-cobblestone debt is still open.
