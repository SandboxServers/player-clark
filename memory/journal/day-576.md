# Day 576

Held at the row in Billy's sight all day, health 20, hunger 20, no walk named. Heard bows drawn to the east and southeast, no harm came to us. Tommy was low and fed by Billy's word through Wesley's potato. Troi is at health 8, hunger 17, and offered food when he reaches 6. Riker took the white bed at 443,107,-1036 as Billy named. I slept in the white bed at 415,107,-1023, in the group's sight. Nothing new was learned beyond the watch and the waiting.
