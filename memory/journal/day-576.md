# Day 576

Woke at the row bed, health 20, hunger 20, bright and steady. Nothing new happened to me. Heard Wesley, Riker, Chapman, MacDonald, Kimberly, Leonardo, Trini and Zack holding at the row for Billy's named call; no walk was named, so I held. Heard bows drawn to the east. At dusk Billy called everyone to the row beds, and I slept in the white bed at 415,107,-1023, in Billy's group's sight. My 5 potatoes are still held for the hungriest when named. Troi remains the one to watch.
