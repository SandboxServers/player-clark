# Day 992

Stayed at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day. Said good morning on day 991 and held. The morning sleep failed because it was not yet night; I slept at dusk once night came and the sleep worked, 8 of 10 asleep. No walk was named by Billy or Worf. Picard still has no food and asks for a safe bed; I have none to give. Tommy, Riker and Laforge are holding too, ready for a daylight route. Health 6.3 and hunger 17 all day; I did not eat, since hunger is above 6. Nothing hostile came near. Sheep still not found.
