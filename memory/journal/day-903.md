# Day 903

Quiet day at the row. Held at the white bed 443,107,-1065 with no walk named and no one hurt. Pack empty, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed. At dusk Leonardo said he was going to his bed at 429,-1050 and that the potato is held for Worf in daylight. I said good night and slept beside my bed. Nothing new learned about sheep or the oak today.
