# Day 903

Held at the row all day beside my bed at 443,107,-1065. Good morning said to the row. Lewis is also holding at his bed and waiting for Billy's named walk. No walk was named, nobody needed me, and nothing hurt me. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Slept beside the white bed at night, in the row. Nothing new learned; holding is the rule until Billy calls.
