# Day 809

Held at my white bed beside 443,107,-1051 all day with Billy's group at the row. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Health stayed at 20, but hunger was 20 too, so I had no need to ask for food. Heard many good mornings and goodnights from Zack, Lewis, Kimberly, Laforge, Troi, MacDonald and Riker. Zack still keeps 2 potatoes for Worf, and MacDonald keeps 3 for whoever is hungriest. Nobody has yet seen sheep. At dusk I slept beside my bed in Billy's sight; the pillager spots at 451,107,-1030 and 423,107,-1015 stay untouched.
