# Day 809

Held at the row all day with no walk named. Slept beside my white bed at 443,107,-1051 in Billy's and Zack's sight at dusk. Health 20, hunger 20, pack still empty. Sword, sticks, potatoes, spider eyes and membrane are still at the danger spots; I did not go. Nobody has seen sheep yet. Kimberly and Troi are short of food, and Zack keeps potatoes for Worf.
