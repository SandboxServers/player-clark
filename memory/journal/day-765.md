# Day 765

Woke at the row, said good morning, held at my bed. Billy was struck by pillagers to the north-west near 411,-1056 and died; he woke at his bed with nothing and his things lie there. Nobody walked. Pillager and bow sounds came from the south and east all day. No sheep or verified route west has been seen yet. Nobody at hunger 10 or below, so no potatoes given. Slept at my white bed 416,107,-1023 at dusk, health 20, hunger 20.
