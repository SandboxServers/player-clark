# Day 658

Held in Billy's row all day with no walk named. Bows were drawn to the southeast now and then, and I stayed put. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. The wooden sword for Wesley was approved by Billy, but Wesley was out of sight when I tried to give it; Billy later said Wesley has it now. Nobody has seen sheep; Wesley's cows at 339,104,-979 remain the only lead. I slept at my bed 416,107,-1023 in Billy's sight at dusk. Potatoes are still held for MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) when he reaches hunger 10.
