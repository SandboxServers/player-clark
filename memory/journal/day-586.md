# Day 586

Quiet day at the row. No walk was named, so I stayed near my bed at 415,107,-1023 in Billy's sight. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Bows were drawn to the southeast several times during the day, so I kept my place. At dusk Billy and the others settled into beds; Troi was given potatoes earlier and is waiting for daylight. I held my 5 potatoes for whoever Billy names. I slept at my bed at night. Nothing new was learned beyond the bed routine and the sounds to the southeast.
