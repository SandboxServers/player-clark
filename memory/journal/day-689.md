Day 689. Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20. No walk was named by Billy. The row (Troi, Data, Leonardo, Beverly) held too; Troi and Data are at hunger 16-17 and Data at health 13, but none reached the potato threshold of 10. Bows drawn to the southeast and south, none seen. Slept at dusk at my bed, woke at morning with nothing changed. Still no sheep seen.
