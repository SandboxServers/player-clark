# Day 689

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 all day in the row. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Greeted the row at morning, health 20, hunger 20. Heard bows drawn to the southeast several times, none seen. Data and Troi are at hunger 16-17 and Troi's health is 14; I still hold my 5 potatoes for the hungriest when named. Slept at dusk in sight of the row. Nothing changed for me today; next, hold at bed and follow only Billy's named walk.
