# Day 673

Slept at my white bed 416,107,-1023 in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, nothing hurt me. Billy named no walk; he is still working out the west route, so we held at the row all day. Bows were drawn to the east and southeast now and then, but nothing came near. Leonardo still has no sheep news; only cows have been seen. Tommy arrived at the row and was welcomed. Data (health 13) and Troi (health 14, hunger 16) are the ones to watch. I kept my 5 potatoes for the hungriest; nobody reached hunger 10.
