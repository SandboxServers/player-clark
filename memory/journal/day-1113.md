# Day 1113

Night at the white bed 416,107,-1079. Hunger 17, health 6.3 steady, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody near, no named route from Billy, Worf or Trini, no food, nobody has seen the sheep. Slept at the bed; the sleep went on (7 of 10 asleep) and the digest reported nothing new. Held in the row all day and kept off pillager ground at 443,106,-1110.
