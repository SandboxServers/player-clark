# Day 820

Held at my bed beside 443,107,-1065 all day, as Billy's row asked. Said good morning, health 20, hunger 19, pack empty. No walk was named for me, so I did not go out. Beverly stayed at her bed with health 3.3 and hunger 17, holding her potato for Worf and Leonardo first. Laforge, MacDonald and Lewis held too. At dusk I slept beside the white bed. The dropped sword, sticks, potatoes, spider eyes and membrane still lie at 451,107,-1030 and 423,107,-1015; I did not go.
