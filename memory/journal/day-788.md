# Day 788

Held at the row all day beside my bed at 443,107,-1037, in Billy's sight. Said good morning. Billy named no walk: no verified route, no sheep seen, food thin. Troi at health 14, hunger 17, has only one potato. Health and hunger stayed at 20; pack still empty. Slept at dusk beside the bed, in sight of the row. Nothing new found; nothing lost.
