# Day 899

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1065 all day, unarmed, no walk named by Billy. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Nothing came near. Heard Troi, Lewis and Riker at the row; Riker is at health 11, hunger 17, and I have no food to give. At night I stood beside the bed in the row's sight and slept. Nobody was hurt today.

Lesson kept: do not walk out alone. Wait for Billy's named call or an armed friend.
