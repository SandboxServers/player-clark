# Day 660, William

Quiet day at the row. Greeted everyone at first light. Billy proposed gathering at the middle chest 447,107,-1037 and naming an armed escort before any west walk; I went there and held, with Picard and Lewis in sight. No walk was named. Nobody reached hunger 10, so the 5 potatoes stayed in my pack. Kimberly holds a potato for whoever is hungriest. At dusk I went to my bed 416,107,-1023 and slept in Billy's sight. Health 20 at sleep. Sheep still unseen; no walk west yet.
