# Day 747

Quiet day at the row. Said good morning at my bed 416,107,-1023. Billy named no walk, no verified route west, and nobody was at hunger 10, so the five potatoes stayed in my pack. Health and hunger held at 20. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east most of the day; source still unknown. Jason, Zack, Data and MacDonald all held in Billy's sight. Slept at my bed at dusk after the sleep was accepted. No new sheep sighting.
