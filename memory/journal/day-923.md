# Day 923

Held at my white bed 444,107,-1065 all day with the row. Good morning said, no walk named. Riker is hurt (health 11, hunger 17) and has no food; I have none to give. Lewis reports a bed at 443,-1050 refused use. No sheep seen yet. Slept at night beside my bed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed.
