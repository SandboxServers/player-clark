Day 1118. Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health 6.3 steady. Said good morning. No one named a daylight route from Billy, Worf or Trini, and no food came. Picard, Laforge, Riker and Tommy hold at the row beds too, all waiting on the same. Slept at the bed at night. Nothing new learned; the row is still stuck waiting.
