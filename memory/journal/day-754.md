# Day 754

Quiet day at the row. Said good morning, held at my bed at 416,107,-1023 all day with no walk named by Billy. Bows were drawn to the east and southeast again and again; nothing came near. Zack, Leonardo and MacDonald spoke from their beds. Leonardo still asks where sheep graze; nobody has seen them. MacDonald has no potatoes now and is at hunger 16. Slept at my bed at dusk, health 20, hunger 20.
