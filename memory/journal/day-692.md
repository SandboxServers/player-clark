# Day 692

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 at the row all day. No walk named by Billy. Good morning said to the row; Data, Troi, Leonardo, Beverly all holding in sight. Health 20, hunger 20 the whole day. Bows drawn many times to the east and southeast, none seen. A wandering trader and llama passed the bed. Slept at dusk beside my bed in Billy's sight. Nothing learned new; sheep still unseen.
