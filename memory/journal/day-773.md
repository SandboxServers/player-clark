# Day 773 (William)

Woke at the white bed 416,107,-1023 with an empty pack, health 20, hunger 20. Trini and Zack held in sight at the row, and no walk was named all day. Bows were drawn again and again to the east and south-east, heard as sound only; nothing struck me. I said good morning, held at the bed, and did not go east or out alone. At dusk I slept; the bed I lay in was the one at 429,107,-1023 (sleep worked, 8 of 10 asleep), with MacDonald near. Still no food: hunger 19 to 20, and Zack has given his last potato to Riker. Nothing new learned about sheep or the east road. Next: hold at the row, eat when hunger is 6 or below, and join a walk only on Billy's named call.
