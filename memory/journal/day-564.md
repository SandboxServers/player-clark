# Day 564

Held at the row (416,107,-1023) all day in Billy's sight. Billy named no walk or food for me. Jason's potato gives to Wesley kept failing because the potatoes lay on the ground by Wesley; Billy stopped them and told Wesley to pick them up. Bows were heard to the east and southeast through the day. At dusk I slept at my bed; the first try covered only 1 of 10 needed, the second worked. I still hold 6 potatoes, 2 spider eyes, 3 sticks, phantom membrane and the wooden sword.
