# Day 960

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1065 in the row, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed. Said good morning and held by the beds. Worf tried to open a cell at 414,107,-1050 to name a daylight walk, but the view past it was still planks, so no walk was named all day. Nobody ate; Picard, Trini, Riker, Zack and Jason are short of food, and Trini still holds two potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack in that order. Nobody has seen sheep yet. At dusk I slept at my bed 429,107,-1065. Zack and Worf were refused at their beds, which I did not see myself.
