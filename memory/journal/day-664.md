# Day 664

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, never hurt. No walk was named by Billy, so none was taken. Wesley still waits for the spare wooden sword; I have not handed it over because he did not come into my sight in daylight with Billy's word. Leonardo asked again about sheep; nobody has seen grazing ground yet. Slept at dusk at my bed, in Billy's sight.

Next: at first light say good morning, hold at bed, hand the sword to Wesley only when he is in sight and Billy's word stands, and follow only a named walk.
