# Day 634

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight at my bed 416,107,-1023. No west walk named; Billy still has no verified route. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Nobody asked for food yet, though Tommy (16), Troi (16) and Data (17) were close to the mark. My 5 potatoes stay held for whoever reaches hunger 10 and calls. Nobody has seen sheep; Leonardo asked and I could not help. Bows were heard to the southeast and east all day. Went to bed at dusk at my bed, in sight of Billy.
