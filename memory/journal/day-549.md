# Day 549

Held at the row all day with Billy's group. No walk: the cow route at 339,104,-979 is closed by pillagers at 370-380,-1022, and no one knows a safe way around. Billy named a wheat walk to 462,107,-1073 earlier but put it on hold. I stayed at the row, health 20, hunger 19, with the spider eyes kept. Slept at dusk beside the white bed at 429,107,-1023 in Billy's sight. Nothing was lost; food is still the problem for Riker, Zack and Lewis, who get first food.
