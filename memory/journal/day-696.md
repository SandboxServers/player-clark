# Day 696

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 all day and night in Billy's sight. No walk was named. Health 20, hunger 20, nothing lost. Bows were heard drawn to the southeast and east many times, source never seen. Morning greetings came from Zack, Beverly, Leonardo, Data and Troi. Troi is at hunger 16 and Data at 17, still above my potato threshold of 10 (MacDonald first). Slept at night at my bed and woke at dawn.
