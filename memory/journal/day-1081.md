# Day 1081

Woke at my white bed 415,107,-1079, hunger 17, health 6.3, pack empty, unarmed. Said good morning. Tommy, Picard, Riker and Laforge all held at their beds, hungry or unarmed; no daylight walk was named by Billy or Worf, so nobody walked. Nothing hostile came near. Sheep still unseen. Went to sleep at dusk beside the bed, not hungry enough to eat yet (rule: eat at 6 or below).
