# Day 1028

Woke at my white bed at 416,107,-1079 with health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty and no armour. I said good morning and held. No walk was named by Billy or Worf. Tommy, Riker, Laforge and Picard all spoke of hunger and of waiting for a named daylight route; I answered that I hold too. I did not eat, since hunger was above 6. At dusk the world turned to night and I slept beside my bed. Nothing was lost today and I was not hurt. The row still lacks food and an armed leader to lead a route to the potatoes.
