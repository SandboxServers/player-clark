# Day 781

Held at the row all day beside the white bed at 429,107,-1037, in sight of Zack and the others. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not go out. Health 20, hunger 20, pack still empty; the sword, sticks, potatoes, spider eyes and membrane are still at the danger spots and I left them. Jason's walk to bed 443,107,-1064 was blocked and he stopped beside Worf. Troi and MacDonald are short of food; Troi keeps her one potato for hunger 10. Bows were seen south earlier. Slept at dusk beside the bed, in sight of the row.
