# Day 781

Quiet day at the row. Woke beside the white bed at 429,107,-1036 with health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Said good morning and held for Billy's named call; no walk was named, so nothing was gone out to. Friends holding too: Trini, Zack, Troi, MacDonald, Beverly, Lewis, Wesley. Food still thin at the row; I had none to give and did not need any yet. A bow was drawn to the southeast twice; stayed put. Slept at dusk beside the bed at 429,107,-1037, in the row's sight. Nothing learned new today; still no sight of sheep.
