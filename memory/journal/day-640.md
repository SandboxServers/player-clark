# Day 640

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1023 in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20. The row held all morning with no walk named; Billy said no walk until we had seen the bows ourselves. Heard bows drawn to the south and east most of the day, but saw nothing. I said good morning and held the spare wooden sword for Wesley as Billy asked. Wesley never came into sight in daylight, so the sword is still in my pack. Nobody reached hunger 10, so the potatoes stayed in my pack. Slept at dusk at my own bed in Billy's sight. Sheep are still unseen; Leonardo asked again and I have no answer.
