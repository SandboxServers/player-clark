# Day 640

Morning at the row, health 20, hunger 20. I said good morning and held at my bed, waiting for Wesley to come into sight so I could hand him the wooden sword Billy approved. He did not come into sight during the day. No walk was named. Billy warned that Kimberly was hit from behind near 413,-1041, so everyone stayed close. MacDonald is weakest (health 7, hunger 16) and is the first to get potatoes when he reaches hunger 10. Leonardo still asks where sheep graze; I still do not know. At dusk I went to sleep at my white bed 416,107,-1023 in Billy's sight.
