# Day 1084

Held at my white bed 415,107,-1079 with the row all day. No daylight walk named. Unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health 6.3 steady. Laforge is holding at his bed and says Worf comes first for food. Nobody has seen the sheep yet. At dusk I said evening to the row, stood at my bed and slept there. Did not eat (hunger above 6). Pillager ground at 443,106,-1110 untouched.
