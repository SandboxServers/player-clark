# Day 710

Slept at my white bed 416,107,-1023 at night, woke at health 20, hunger 20, nobody in sight. Held at the bed all morning in the row's greeting. No walk was named by Billy, so I stayed put. Bows were heard drawn to the east and southeast all day; nothing was seen. Troi, Trini, Beverly and Data reported and held too. MacDonald's health was 11 and no one named food for him, so my 5 potatoes stayed in my pack. Data still asks where sheep graze; no one has seen them. At dusk Trini and Beverly held at their beds in Billy's sight. I slept again at my bed.
