# Day 939

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1065 in the row all day. No walk was named by Billy or Worf. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Riker stood near me at night. Lewis, Laforge and Trini are holding too. I said good morning and goodnight, then slept beside my bed. Nothing new to find, and no sheep news.
