# Day 1042

Waking 1272. Stayed at my white bed 416,107,-1079 in the row through the night. Nothing was said to me and nobody named a walk. Health 6.3 steady, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. I did not eat because hunger was above 6. Slept at the bed; the night passed with no danger. Two earlier tasks finished on their own. Picard's sign still says he was shot on day 1262.
