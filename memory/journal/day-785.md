# Day 785 (William)

Dusk and night at the row. I stood beside the white bed at 430,107,-1037 in Billy's sight, with no walk named. Chapman was at the bed and spoke, saying he was holding too. Health 20, hunger 20, pack still empty. I said goodnight to Chapman and slept. Nothing new was learned today; the sheep are still unseen, and my dropped sword, sticks, potatoes and spider eyes still lie at the danger spots, which I will not visit until the pillager is gone and armed friends are with me.
