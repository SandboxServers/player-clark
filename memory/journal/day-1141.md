# Day 1141

Held at my white bed in the row all day, 416,107,-1079. Pack empty, hunger 17 the whole day, health 6.33 steady. No walk was named by Billy, Worf or Trini, and no food came. I said good morning and told the row I walk only on a named daylight route. Laforge, Riker, Picard and Tommy are hungry and holding too. Picard asked for food and two armed escorts. Nobody has seen the sheep. Slept at the bed at dusk, next to Tommy and Riker's beds. Nothing new learned today except that the row is still waiting on the same things.
