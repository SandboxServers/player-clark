# Day 906

Dusk at my bed 443,107,-1065 at the row. Lewis and Riker held beside their beds too, no walks in the dark. I said I was holding and no walk was named. Night came and I slept beside the bed on the first call, in sight of the row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Nothing hurt me and nothing new came up. Still no sheep seen.
