# Day 1025

Held all day at the row beside my white bed 416,107,-1079. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, and neither answered. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 6.3, pack empty, unarmed. Picard, Tommy, Riker and Laforge also hold at their beds, all waiting for a named daylight route with open cells to the potatoes at 463-470,107,-1068. Tommy asked for a sword or wood. Nothing was given or taken. I slept at dusk beside the bed; it took a few tries before the sleep held. Nothing new was learned except that the row is still quiet and food is still short.
