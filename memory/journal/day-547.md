# Day 547

Night at the bed row in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 19, no food but two spider eyes, which Billy says to keep since they poison and do not feed. The west is closed and fewer than seven are armed, so no walk was called. Lewis, at health 7, is first in line for any real food. I held in sight, said my piece, and went to bed beside the white bed at 429,107,-1023.
