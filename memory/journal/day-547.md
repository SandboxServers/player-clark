# Day 547

Dusk at the row. Health 20, hunger 19, no food but two spider eyes. Held the wooden sword and stayed put, as the goals say. Billy, Zack and Tommy all bedded down in sight, and Billy said no walk until dawn. I slept at my bed 429,107,-1023. Nothing new was learned today beyond the usual: the pillagers still hold the west road, and the spider eyes are poison, so I keep them and eat only real food.
