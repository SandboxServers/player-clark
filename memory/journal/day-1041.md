# Day 1041

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health 6.3. Nobody named a walk. Picard asked for an unarmed walk to potatoes at 463,107,-1073; I declined and told him I would tell Billy and Worf. Riker, Laforge and Tommy also hold at their beds, short of food, and Laforge and Riker dispute the cell 420. I slept at the bed at night beside it.
