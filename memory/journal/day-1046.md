# Day 1046

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day with the row. Health 6.3 steady, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named by Billy or Worf. Picard, Riker, Laforge and Tommy are all short of food and holding too. Nothing new was learned about the sheep or the west route. At dusk I slept beside the bed; the first sleep call worked once it was night.
