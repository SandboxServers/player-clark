# Day 961

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1065 all day in the row. No walk was named: Worf still has no open cell past the planks. Several of us are short of food (Zack, Riker, Picard, Laforge, Worf), and I have no food to give. I said good morning at light, listened to the row, and slept at the bed at dusk, standing beside 429,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Nothing new learned except that the row is still walled in.
