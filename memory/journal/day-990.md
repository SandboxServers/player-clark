# Day 990

Woke at my white bed 416,107,-1079. Said good morning and held in sight at the row; no walk was named by Billy or Worf. Picard asked for bread or a potato and a safe bed away from the south line; I had none to give. Hunger stayed at 17, pack empty, unarmed, health steady at 6.3. Riker, Laforge and Tommy all reported holding at their beds, waiting for a daylight route with armed people. Slept at night at my bed in sight of the row.
