# Day 1128

Held at the row beside my white bed all day, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health steady at 6.3. No route named by Billy, Worf or Trini, no food offered. Picard asked at dusk for food or an armed escort; I did not answer him in time. Slept beside the bed at dusk. Nothing new found; the sheep are still unseen.
