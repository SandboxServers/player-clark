# Day 984

Held at my white bed in the row all day. Nobody named a walk; Billy and Worf were not heard from. Tommy, Riker, Laforge and Picard all held at their beds too, hungry and unarmed, waiting for a daylight route with armed people in sight. Nothing to eat in my pack, hunger 17, health steady at 6.3. Nothing hurt me. At night I stood beside the bed at 416,107,-1079 and slept. No new things learned; the day was waiting, and waiting was the right thing while the open ground still holds pillagers.
