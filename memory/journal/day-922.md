# Day 922

Held at my white bed 444,107,-1065 in the row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, so I did not leave. Said good morning to the row, and told Riker I have seen no sheep either. Lewis, Riker and Laforge are all holding in the row, unarmed, with no food. Worf made a north-east walk for potatoes without naming me, and I stayed put. At night I lay down at my bed; sleep was partly taken (6 of 10) and was still settling when this was written.
