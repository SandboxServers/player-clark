# Day 1093

Woke at my white bed 416,107,-1079 with health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty and no food. Said good morning on the speech line. Nobody named a daylight route all day. Tommy and Picard both asked for food and an escort; I had nothing to give. Picard holds at the north bed 416,107,-1093. Night fell, I stood beside my bed and slept there successfully. Nothing new learned except that the row is still holding and the sheep are still unseen. Hunger is high but I do not eat above 6.
