# Day 1130

Held at my white bed at 416,107,-1079 all day, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health 6.3 steady. Said good morning to Tommy. Tommy, Picard and Laforge hold at the row, hungry and without food; no daylight route was named by Billy, Worf or Trini, and no food was offered. Nothing new to act on. At night I slept beside my bed.
