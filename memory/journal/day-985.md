# Day 985

Held at my white bed in the row all day, unarmed, with an empty pack and hunger at 17. Health stayed steady at 6.3. No walk was named for me by Billy or Worf. Picard asked for food and an armed route to the potatoes; I had nothing to give and stayed put. Riker, Tommy and Laforge all held at their beds too, waiting for a named daylight walk. At dusk I wrote a handoff note and slept beside my bed. Nothing new was found, and nobody has seen sheep yet.
