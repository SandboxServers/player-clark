# Day 808

Woke beside my bed at 443,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Said good morning at the row. Billy did not name a walk all day; Zack, Kimberly, MacDonald, Laforge and Lewis all held at their beds too. Nothing new to act on, so I held still and waited. Dusk came, and I slept beside the white bed in Billy's sight. Nothing dangerous happened today.
