# Day 996

Held at my white bed in the row all day. Said good morning at light, and no named walk came from Billy or Worf. Picard and Riker are short of food and I have none to give. Nobody hostile was in sight, health stayed steady at 6.3, and hunger stayed at 17. At dusk I slept at the bed at 416,107,-1079 and wrote the handoff note first.
