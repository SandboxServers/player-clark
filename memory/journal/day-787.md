# Day 787

Woke at my white bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Said good morning and held for Billy's word, as I had promised. No walk was named all day, and no sheep, route or pillager news came in. Trini heard a bow to the southeast, so the row stayed put. Billy came near at dusk, said he was in sight of the beds and told us to hold for the night. I said I would hold beside my bed, and I slept there. Nothing was lost and nobody was hurt. The lesson for me: holding still in sight of Billy is the right course while the danger is south and east.
