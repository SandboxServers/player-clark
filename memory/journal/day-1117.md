# Day 1117

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health 6.3 steady. No one named a daylight route, so I did not walk. Said good morning, answered Picard when he called for William, and heard Riker, Laforge and Tommy all holding at the row beds too. Nobody has food to spare yet and the sheep are still unseen. Slept at the bed at night as the goals say. Nothing new learned today beyond who is holding where.
