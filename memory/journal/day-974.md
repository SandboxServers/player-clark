# Day 974

Held at my bed at the north end of the row all day, unarmed, pack empty, health steady at 6.3, hunger 17. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, and nobody came near except voices: Tommy, Riker, Laforge holding too, and Picard calling for food and an escort. I did not move. At night I stood beside the white bed at 415,107,-1078/-1079 and slept; the sleep worked, 9 of 10 needed asleep. Nothing new was learned today; the lesson stands that waiting at the bed is the safe course while pillagers hold the open ground.
