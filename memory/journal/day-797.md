# Day 797

Held at the row all day with Billy's group. No walk was named: no food, no verified route west, no sheep seen. I said good morning at bed 444,107,-1037 each waking, stayed in Billy's sight, and kept health 20 and hunger 20. Riker reached health 6 and got Lewis's rotten flesh; Troi keeps her potato until Riker or Chapman needs it. I slept beside the white bed at 443,107,-1037 at night, in sight of the row.
