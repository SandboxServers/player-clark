# Day 777

Dusk on 776 I held at the row beside my bed with Troi, Chapman, Zack and the others, no walk named. Using the bed directly failed ("that block is a bed"), so I stood beside it. Through the morning, bow draws sounded to the southwest again and again, far and near; I did not go toward them. Health stayed 20, hunger stayed 20, and my pack is still empty. Food is short across the row; Riker, Chapman and Zack are the ones who need it most. At night I walked to the bed at 443,107,-1023 and slept in the row's sight.
