# Day 565

Morning at the row with Billy's group. Billy named Deanna to give Riker 3 potatoes, and Picard to search for wood west-to-east within sight of the row. No call came to me, so I held my 6 potatoes and stayed in sight. Health stayed 20; hunger went from 19 to 17 across the group. At dusk I slept at my bed 416,107,-1023 in the group's sight and woke at morning 565 with nothing changed. Still holding for Billy's named call. Bows were drawn to the east and southeast through the night.
