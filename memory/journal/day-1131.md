# Day 1131

Held at my white bed at 416,107,-1079 all day, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health 6.3 steady. No daylight route named by Billy, Worf or Trini, and no food offered. Tommy, Picard and Laforge also hold at the row, hungry. I told Tommy I had no stick or sword to spare. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
