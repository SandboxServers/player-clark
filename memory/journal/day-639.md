# Day 639

Quiet day at the row with Billy's group. No walk was named, so we held. Nobody was at hunger 10 or below, so no potatoes went out. Billy approved handing my spare wooden sword to Wesley in daylight, but Wesley waits for the row to arm him before he comes to me, and he was not in my sight by dusk. I kept the sword for him. Slept at my bed 416,107,-1023 in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20. Still no verified west route, and nobody has seen sheep yet.
