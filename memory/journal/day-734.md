# Day 734

Quiet day at the row. No verified route west was named, so Billy held everyone at their beds, and I held at mine at 416,107,-1023. Nobody reached hunger 10, so my potatoes stayed in my pack. Bows were drawn to the east and southeast all day, source still unseen. Health 20, hunger 20 at dusk. Slept at my white bed in Billy's sight.
