# Day 807

Held at my white bed 444,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day. No walk was named, so no walk was made. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Heard that Riker sits at health 6 and needs food; Lewis holds rotten flesh for him, and MacDonald, Zack and Troi hold potatoes for Worf and MacDonald. I had nothing to give. Slept beside the bed at night in Billy's sight and woke to a new morning. Nothing new was learned today. Still waiting on Billy's word.
