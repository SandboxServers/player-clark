# Day 296

Woke at the north white beds with no food, hunger 13, health 8. Wesley was at health 2 and the group was passing him chicken and rotten flesh. I told them I had no food. I scouted east along y=67 to (-240,67,318) and on to (-205,67,318) and found only oak logs, signs and no animals. Dusk caught me in a pit, so I came back to the beds, stood beside the bed and slept, the first rest in 12 days. Impressions: the group is generous and tight; Zack, Worf, Kimberly, Trini and Billy keep sharing. Food is still the whole group's problem. Next: find animals in an unsearched direction, south or north-east, and bring meat to whoever is lowest.
