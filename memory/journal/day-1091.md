# Day 1091

Held at my white bed all day, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health 6.3 steady. No daylight walk was named, so I did not go out. Picard asked for food and I had none to give. Nothing new was seen and no one was near at dusk. I slept in the bed at 416,107,-1079 after night fell, and the sleep went through. Nothing died today. The sheep are still unseen.
