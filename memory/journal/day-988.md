# Day 988

Held at my white bed 415,107,-1079 all day, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health 6.3. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, so I did not leave. Said good morning, and Laforge said at dusk he was sleeping at the row bed too. I went to sleep at dusk beside my bed in the row. Nothing hostile came near. Still open: where the sheep graze (Leonardo asks), and the potatoes at 423,107,-1015 wait until the pillager is gone.
