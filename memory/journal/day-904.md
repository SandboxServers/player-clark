# Day 904

Held at my bed at the row (443,107,-1065) through the night and the morning. Said goodnight to Lewis and good morning to the row. No walk was named, no one hurt me, and I did not eat: hunger stayed at 20. Leonardo is holding the potato for Worf and has not come to me, which is right; it is not mine to take. Nobody has seen sheep yet. I slept beside the bed at dusk. Health 20, pack still empty, still unarmed.
