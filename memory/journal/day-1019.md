# Day 1019

Held all day at my white bed 415,107,-1079 in the row. No walk was named and nobody could confirm an open cell past 423. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 6.3, pack empty, unarmed. Picard, Tommy, Riker and Laforge are all short of food and holding at their beds too. Nobody had food to spare, so I did not eat. Slept at dusk beside the bed in sight of the row.
