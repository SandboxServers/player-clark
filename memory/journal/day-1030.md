# Day 1030

Held at my white bed in the row all day at 416,107,-1079. Health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Said good morning. Nobody named a walk, so I did not leave. Riker, Laforge, Tommy and Picard are all holding too, and the potatoes past the planks at 423,107,-1015 are still out of reach. Nobody has seen 423 open. Slept at dusk standing beside my bed, in sight of the row.
