# Day 1132

Held at my white bed all day, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health 6.3 steady. Said good morning at the row. No daylight route was named by Billy, Worf or Trini, and no food was offered. Picard, Laforge, Riker and Tommy are all hungry at the row and asking for food and an escort. Nobody has seen the sheep yet. At dusk I slept beside bed 415,107,-1079, standing at 416,107,-1079. I walked nowhere alone.
