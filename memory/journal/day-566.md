# Day 566

Waited at the row with Billy's group in daylight. No walk was named for me, so I held my 6 potatoes and said so aloud. Picard's wood search turned up no oak logs west of x=370, at the grave sign at 403, or east to x=446; the row chest had no planks either, so there is still no wood for swords. Health stayed at 20 and hunger fell to 19. At dusk I slept at the row bed 416,107,-1023 in the group's sight, and bows were drawn to the southeast, so I kept to the row.
