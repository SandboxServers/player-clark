# Day 772

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1023 all day in Billy's sight. No walk was named. Health 20, hunger 20, pack still empty. Zack, Leonardo, MacDonald and Data all waited at the row. Riker is low on health with no food, and Zack holds 2 potatoes for him and for MacDonald and Worf. I have nothing to give. Bows were drawn to the southeast and south through the day and into dusk, source still unseen. Sheep are still unseen. My first use of the bed was refused at dusk; sleep alone worked. Slept at night at my bed.
