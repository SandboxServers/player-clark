# Day 681

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 all day in Billy's row. No walk was named, so none was taken. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Bows drawn to the southeast and east, no one hurt. Leonardo reported cows far east near 339,104,-979; still no one has seen sheep, so goal 7 stays open. Troi is at hunger 16 and will eat at 10. Slept at my bed at dusk with nothing half-done.
