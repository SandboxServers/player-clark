# Day 1038

Held at my white bed at 416,107,-1079 all day with nobody naming a walk. Health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Picard, Riker, Laforge and Tommy also hold at their beds, short of food. Picard said he is asleep in his north bed. I slept beside my bed at night and the sleep worked. Nothing new was found; the sheep are still unseen.
