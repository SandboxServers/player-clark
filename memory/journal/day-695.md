# Day 695

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 all day in Billy's sight. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Said good morning at first light. Heard bows drawn to the east and southeast, source still unknown. Data (hunger 17) and Troi (hunger 16) are the hungriest; the potato call comes at hunger 10. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep. Slept at the white bed at night in the row's sight.
