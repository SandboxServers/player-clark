# Day 800

Held at the row all day beside my bed at 443,107,-1037, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Billy named no walk: no verified route to the blocked tile on the north-east route, and bows were heard to the south. MacDonald is at hunger 16 with no food and has said he will call me at hunger 10. I slept beside the bed at night with Billy and Zack in sight. Nothing new learned, and the sheep question is still open.
