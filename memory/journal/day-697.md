# Day 697

Quiet day at the row. I held at my bed 416,107,-1023 from first light, greeted the row, and heard no walk named by Billy. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east several times; none were seen. Troi and Data were hurt and near hunger 16-17, short of the hunger 10 that calls for my potatoes. Zack offered them potatoes too. Leonardo still has no word on sheep. I slept at dusk in Billy's sight; the first sleep came back partial (6 of 10), then the night was rested. Health 20, hunger 20 at the end of the day.
