Day 1047. Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day. Health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody named a daylight walk, so I did not go out. I said good morning and heard the row (Tommy, Laforge, Riker, Picard) also holding; Riker says Trini has the potatoes and Worf and Zack come first. Slept at night beside my bed. Nothing new learned; the sheep are still unseen.
