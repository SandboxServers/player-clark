# Day 1152

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day, unarmed, pack empty. Hunger 17, health 6.33 steady. Said good morning twice. No walk was named by Billy, Worf or Trini and no food came. New arrivals (Scotty, Kira, Adam, Gimli) are also empty; Picard and Riker hold at the north beds. Aragorn reported a pillager on him to the north-west and ran. Sleep was attempted at dusk beside the bed; the bed count showed 12 of 25 asleep. The sheep are still unfound.
