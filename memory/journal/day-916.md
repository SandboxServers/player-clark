# Day 916

Held at my white bed 444,107,-1065 in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Nobody named a daylight walk, so I did not go out. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Riker, Laforge and Lewis hold in the row too; Riker and Laforge are both short of food and have no swords. Two row beds at 429 refused Laforge at night, and Lewis's bed at 443,107,-1051 refused him earlier. Still no sheep seen. Slept at my bed tonight.
