# Day 1143

Held at my white bed at 416,107,-1079 all day. Pack empty, hunger 17, health 6.33 steady. No walk named by Billy, Worf or Trini, no food came, and I did not go out alone. Slept at the bed at dusk; the sleep took 7 of 10 needed. Nothing new learned. Nobody has seen the sheep yet, so Leonardo is still waiting.
