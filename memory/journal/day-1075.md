# Day 1075

Held at my white bed at 416,107,-1079 all day with the row. No daylight walk was named by Billy or Worf, and I had no food (hunger 17, health 6.3) and nothing in my pack. Picard asked at the north bed for anyone to answer; I said I was here. Tommy is holding until morning and also has no food or sword. I slept at night at the bed. Sheep still unseen.
