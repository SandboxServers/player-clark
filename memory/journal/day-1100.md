# Day 1100

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day and night, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health 6.3 steady. Nobody named a daylight walk, so I did not leave the row. Picard, Riker, Tommy and Laforge are all hungry at nearby beds and asking for food and an armed escort; I have nothing to give. I told Picard I have no food and will walk when Billy or Worf names a route. No one answered Picard's calls during the day. I slept at the bed at dusk, standing beside it, as the goals say.
