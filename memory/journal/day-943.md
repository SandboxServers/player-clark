# Day 943

I slept at my white bed 429,107,-1065 in the row, sheltered, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named by Billy or Worf all day, so I held at the bed. In the morning I said good morning; Riker and Picard, Lewis and Trini are short of food and some are hurt (Riker 11, Picard 2.3). I have no food to give yet, since my potatoes are still at 423,107,-1015. Nothing new was learned about sheep. The row is holding, and the pillagers are still out on the open ground.
