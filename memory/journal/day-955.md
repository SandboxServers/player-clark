Day 955. Held at my white bed 430,107,-1065 in the row all day and night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Said goodnight and good morning. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, so I did not leave. Zack and Trini are short of food and waiting for the same call. Lewis and Jason hold at their beds too. Nothing new was found. Slept at dusk beside the bed.
