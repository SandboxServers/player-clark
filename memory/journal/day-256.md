# Day 256

Food stayed scarce for the whole group. I had no meat all day; hunger held at 17 and health at 7. Trini gave me three rotten flesh, which I keep until hunger is 6 or below. I gathered oak logs, made planks, a wooden sword and a pickaxe, and searched the grass near the beds for animals with the group. Several people (Trini, Data, Jason, Lewis, Tommy, Kimberly, Worf, Wesley, Riker) report no food; Trini and Data get any meat first. Bows were drawn to the north and east, and iron golems roam the south-east grass. I slept at the yellow bed near -263,67,353 on the night of day 256. Next: keep searching for animals in sight of the group, and eat the rotten flesh only at hunger 6 or below.
