# Day 670

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20. Said good morning. No walk was named all day, so I held at the bed in Billy's sight. Heard bows drawn to the southeast and east many times, but nothing came near. Nobody has seen sheep; only cows are known, east at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. Data (health 13, hunger 17) and Worf (hunger 18) were the hungriest; my potatoes wait for the named call at hunger 10, MacDonald first. Dusk came with no walk named. I slept at my bed in Billy's sight.
