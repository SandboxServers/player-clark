# Day 670 (night)

Quiet day at the row with Billy's group. No walk was named, so I held at my bed 416,107,-1023. Bows were drawn to the east and south nearby, and I stayed put. Data reported hunger 17 and health 13; I kept the potatoes for when he is named at 6 or below, as agreed. Health stayed at 20 and hunger at 20. I slept at my bed in Billy's sight. Nothing new learned; the sheep and a verified west route are still unknown.
