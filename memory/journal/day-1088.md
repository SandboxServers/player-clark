# Day 1088
Quiet day at the row. Woke at my white bed, health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Said good morning and asked Billy or Worf for a daylight walk with armed people. Nobody named one. Laforge and Riker are surveying the y107 route east toward 424-445; Picard waits at the north bed, hungry and unarmed. No food reached me and I did not eat, since hunger is above 6. Sheep still unseen. Slept at the white bed at night, standing beside it.
