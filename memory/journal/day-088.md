# Day 088 (1088)

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all dusk. Health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No daylight walk was named. Picard, Laforge and Riker are all hungry and holding till first light; Riker says the first walk is for potatoes at 463-470. Slept at the white bed at night. Nothing new learned about the sheep.
