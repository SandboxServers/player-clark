# Day 574

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight. No walk was named; the oak lead is still unknown. Troi is at health 8, hunger 17, and I offered him potatoes; Billy will call him when he reaches hunger 6. Bows were drawn to the east and southeast all day with none in sight, so we stayed put. Slept at the row bed 416,107,-1023 at dusk with health 20 and hunger 20, nothing lost.
