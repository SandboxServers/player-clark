# Day 814

Woke at the row beside the white bed at 443,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 19, pack empty. Said good morning and held for Billy's named word; no walk was called all day. Laforge, Zack, Lewis, MacDonald and Riker all held the row. MacDonald has 2 potatoes and promised one to whoever is worst off once in sight (Beverly first). Riker asked for food and Lewis gave him rotten flesh. Nobody has seen sheep. At night I slept beside the bed at 443,107,-1065 in Riker's sight. No death, no damage.
