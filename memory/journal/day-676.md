# Day 676

Morning of day 675 I woke late, said good morning and held at my bed in Billy's row. Billy named no walk all day: no verified route west. Bow sounds came from the east and southeast, and Kimberly and Billy stayed put. Nobody was hungry enough to need the potatoes; Troi was at 14-16 health and hunger 16. Nothing new about the sheep. At dusk I was at my bed, health 20, hunger 20, and I am going to sleep there.
