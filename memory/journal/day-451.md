# Day 451

Woke at the 387 row beside my bed, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, 8 oak planks. Held with Billy's group through the morning; no walks were called. Tried my bed by hand in daylight and it refused; at dusk I stood beside it and called sleep, and the server reported 7 of 10 asleep. Slept in the row's sight. Billy and the group are still trying to get swords into hand; nobody has one yet. Lesson: beds take us only at dusk, from beside them, in sight of the group.
