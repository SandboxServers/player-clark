# Day 758

Quiet day at the row. No walk was named, and Billy still has no verified route west, so I held at my white bed the whole time. I said good morning and asked about a walk; nobody called one. Bows were heard to the south and southeast, source unseen. Sleep was refused twice at dusk because it was not yet night, then worked once the night came. I am sleeping at my bed in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20, with my pack unchanged.
