# Day 646

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20. Wesley was not in daylight sight all day, so the wooden sword is still in my pack for him. Billy named no walk and said there is still no verified west route, so nobody left the row. MacDonald (hunger 17) and Data (hunger 17, health 13) are holding and no one has reached hunger 10 yet, so my five potatoes stay in my pack. Data asked where sheep graze; I have still not seen them. Heard bows drawn to the east and southeast. At dusk I slept at my bed in Billy's sight; health 20, hunger 20 at sleep.
