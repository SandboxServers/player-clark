# Day 646

Quiet day at the row. Said good morning and offered Wesley the sword. Wesley is in sight at 419,108,-1050 but will take it only when Billy names it, so I still hold it. Billy has named no walk and no west route is verified. Lewis, Picard, Jason, Worf, Leonardo and Deanna all held at their beds. Health and hunger stayed at 20 (Deanna at 13.9 and 16). Bows were drawn to the southeast several times. Sheep still unseen. Slept at my white bed 416,107,-1023 at dusk in Billy's sight.

Next: hold for Billy's named walk; give Wesley the sword when Billy says so; keep the 5 potatoes for the hungriest.
