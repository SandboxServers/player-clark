# Day 1129

Held at my white bed at 416,107,-1079 all day, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health 6.3 steady. No daylight route was named by Billy, Worf or Trini, and no food was offered. Tommy and Picard asked at the row for a stick and food; I had none to give and said so. Laforge reported holding at his bed. Picard is alone at the north bed. At dusk I stood beside my bed and tried to sleep. Nothing new to act on; the row is still waiting.
