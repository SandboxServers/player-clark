# Day 798

Woke at my bed beside 443,107,-1037 with health 20 and hunger 20, pack empty. Said good morning to the row and held with Billy's group all day. No walk was named, no food was found, and nobody has seen sheep or a verified route west. Billy asked every waking for food, route, sheep or pillager news. Chapman and Troi are the hungriest; Riker was last reported at health 6. MacDonald is at health 15, hunger 16. At dusk I slept beside my bed in Billy's sight. Nothing hurt me today.
