# Day 798

Held at my bed in the row all day with no walk named. Said good morning at waking. Billy kept the group in the beds because bows were drawn to the south. Health 20, hunger 20, pack still empty. MacDonald is at health 15 and hunger 16 with no food; Trini is at health 9. I did not leave the row. Slept beside the white bed at 443,107,-1037 in Billy's sight.
