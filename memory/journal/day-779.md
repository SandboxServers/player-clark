# Day 779

Woke at the white bed 429,107,-1023 at dawn with health 20, hunger 20 and an empty pack. Said good morning to the row. Held all day at the bed for Billy's named walk, which never came. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, never close. Trini, Troi, MacDonald, Chapman, Crusher and Zack all held with me and all are short of food; I had nothing to give. At dusk I stood beside the bed in sight of the others and slept. Nothing new was learned today except that the row is holding steady and waiting.
