# Day 779

Slept beside the white bed at 429,107,-1023 in sight of Trini and MacDonald. Health 20, hunger 20 all night, pack still empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast; nothing came near. Chapman, Troi, Zack, Crusher and Trini are all short of food and holding at the row for Billy's named call. No walk was named, and I did not walk. Sheep are still unseen.
