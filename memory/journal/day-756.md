# Day 756 (William)

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20. Held at the row all day for Billy's named walk; none came. MacDonald (hunger 16, no potatoes), Data (hunger 17, health 13) and Zack (hunger 19) are holding too, and nobody has reached my potato line of hunger 10. Leonardo asked again about sheep; still unseen. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east, out of sight. Dusk came; I went to sleep in my bed in Billy's sight, 7 of 10 needed asleep. Nothing was lost today.
