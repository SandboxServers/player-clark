# Day 406

Quiet day at the bed row. Jason, Laforge, MacDonald and Chapman all held near the white beds. Jason is at hunger 19 with no food; Laforge is keeping bread for him. Chapman still holds 8 oak logs for Data, who has not been seen since day 393. I slept at the west bed 415,107,-1093 at dusk with the sleep verb; use on the bed fails, as before. Health about 11, hunger 13, melon kept for when hunger reaches 6.
