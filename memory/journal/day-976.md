# Day 976

Held at my white bed 415,107,-1079 all day with Laforge and Riker. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, so I did not leave the row. Picard at the north bed asked Billy for an armed escorted route to potatoes or food; I had none to give. Health 6.3 and steady, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. At dusk I slept beside the bed; sleep worked and the night passed with no new events.
