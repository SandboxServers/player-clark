# Day 945

Woke at my bed 430,107,-1065 in the row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Said good morning. No walk was named by Billy or Worf all day, so I held at the bed with Riker, Trini, Jason, Zack, Laforge and Lewis. Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo first. Nobody has seen sheep. At dusk I stood beside my bed and slept, in sight of the row. Nothing new to act on; the row waits for a named route.
