# Day 737

Quiet day at the row. Billy named no walk: nobody has a verified route west past x=370, and no one has seen sheep. Cows were seen at 339,104,-979 by Data. Billy gave Wesley the bed square 415,107,-1023 to hold in sight. Everyone stayed at their beds; hunger stayed above 10 for all, so no potatoes were needed. I said good morning, held at my bed 416,107,-1023, and slept there at night in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep.
