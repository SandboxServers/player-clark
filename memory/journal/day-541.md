# Day 541

Held at the row by my bed 429,107,-1036 in Billy's sight all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 19. The west oak walk was on for the armed four and I stayed put, as my goal says. Billy offered me his spare wooden sword and asked me to come within reach; I was not in his sight when he called, so he kept it. At dusk he said the sword was going to me, but it never reached my pack. Slept at my bed at dusk in Billy's sight. Next morning: check for the sword, then hold for Billy's call.
