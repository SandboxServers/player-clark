# Journal
- 07:45 act: said good morning at the row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Holding at my bed, no walk.
- Day passed holding at bed. Lewis gave a potato to Riker; Kimberly, Zack, MacDonald, Troi have no food. Bows drawn to the south all day. Night came; I am at bed 429,107,-1023 in Billy's sight. Going to sleep.
