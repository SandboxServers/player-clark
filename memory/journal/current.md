# Journal
- Day 927 morning: said good morning at bed 444,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. Nobody near, no walk named. Holding at the row; Riker is hungry (17) and asks for sheep and sword material too.
- Day 928: held at bed 444,107,-1065 all morning, nobody in sight, no walk named. Slept beside the bed at night and woke on day 929. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. Riker and Picard hungry; nothing to give.
