# Journal

- Day 570 morning. Woke at the row bed 416,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 19. Billy held us at the row, no walk. Riker ate a potato on Billy's word.
- Dusk 570. Billy named me to eat one potato; ate it, hunger 19 to 20. 5 potatoes still held for the hungriest. Going to bed at 416,107,-1023 in the row's sight.
