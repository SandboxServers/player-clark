# Journal
- Night, day 1047. Woke at my white bed 416,107,-1079. Health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty. Nobody near. Holding at the bed till morning; not eating (hunger above 6), not walking.
- Morning, day 1048. Said good morning. Picard asked for food and an armed escort to potatoes at 463,107,-1073; I told him I am unarmed and wait for Billy or Worf. Riker, Laforge hold. Still no named walk. Holding at my bed, hunger 17.
- 03:23 say: {"text":"William, night. Holding at my white bed, no walk in the dark. Good night, all."}
- Day 1049 dawn. Heard Picard, Laforge, Tommy hold at beds, asking Billy and Worf for food and a named route. Nobody named one for me yet. Said good morning; holding at bed, hunger 17.
