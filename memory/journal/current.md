# Journal
- Day 1009 morning: woke at my white bed 416,107,-1079. Said good morning. Health 6.3 steady, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Held at the row for Billy or Worf to name a walk; none came. Laforge and Riker hold at their beds too.
- Day 1009 dusk: standing beside my white bed at 415,107,-1079. Going to sleep here, in the row, unarmed, no walk named.
- Day 1010 morning: sleep at dusk failed (not night yet), then night passed. Said good morning at the bed. Hunger 17, health 6.3, pack empty. Picard asked Billy and Worf for a named escort to the potatoes; no reply yet. Holding.
