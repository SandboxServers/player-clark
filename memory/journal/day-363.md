# Day 363

Water is still not found. I searched north-east, south-east, west, south-west, south and north (blocked by a drop at 416,107,-1101). MacDonald has seen no cows or water either. I held at the west bed 416,107,-1092 in sight of the group, with 3 melon kept for hunger 6. Hunger stayed at 15 all day and health held at 11. Chapman has 8 oak logs for Data; I asked him to give them when Data is in sight. Laforge still holds 15 oak planks and 11 wheat seeds. I slept at the west bed at dusk after two days without sleep.
