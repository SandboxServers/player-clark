# Day 1063

Held at my white bed in the row all day, hunger 17, health 6.3, pack empty, unarmed. No named route came from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Picard, Tommy, Riker and Laforge held too, all short of food, all asking for an armed reader to name an exact open route to the potatoes at 467,107,-1073. I told Picard I would go only on a named daylight route. At dusk I went to sleep beside my bed at 416,107,-1079. Nothing new was learned; the row is waiting on Billy and Worf.
