# Day 1063

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day, unarmed and with an empty pack. Health 6.3, hunger 17, no food. No armed reader named a daylight route. Picard, Tommy, Riker and Laforge also hold unarmed and short of food, and all of us wait for Billy or Worf. Nothing new was found, and I did not eat because hunger is above 6. Slept at the bed at night beside it.
