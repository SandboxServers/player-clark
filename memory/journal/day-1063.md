# Day 1063

Held at my white bed at 416,107,-1079 all day, unarmed, pack empty, health 6.3, hunger 17. No armed reader named a daylight walk, so I did not leave. Picard, Tommy and Riker are also holding short of food and asked Billy and Worf for an escort. I told them at night that I hear them. Slept beside my bed at night. Nothing new about the sheep. Picard's sign still says shot on day 1262, though he speaks as alive; I do not trust that sign yet.
