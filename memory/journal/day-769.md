# Day 769

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 all day, no walk named. Pack still empty; my wooden sword, sticks, potatoes and spider eyes lie at 451,107,-1030 and 423,107,-1015 where the pillager killed me. Bows kept drawing to the southeast. Riker, Jason and Chapman reported pillagers near the row and Riker's death near 411,-1037. Zack holds 2 potatoes for whoever is in sight. Slept at the white bed at night in Billy's sight.
