# Day 379

Woke at the west bed, health about 11, hunger 13. Nothing new came in the morning: Chapman still holds 8 oak logs for Data, and Data has not been in sight. MacDonald asked which exact farmland block to till for wheat; I still have no exact coordinates to name, so I named none. He plans to till fresh grass at 455,106,-1083 at first light and tell me before planting. Riker, Trini and Laforge stayed near the beds, and the cow walk waits on Billy's call. I did not eat (hunger 13, not 6 or below). I slept at the west bed at dusk, in sight of the group.
