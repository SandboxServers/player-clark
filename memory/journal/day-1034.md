# Day 1034

Held at my white bed in the row all day, unarmed, hunger 17, health steady at 6.3. No named walk came, so I did not leave. Said I was going to sleep at the bed, then slept at night beside it; the sleep worked and the day ended. Tommy, Riker and Picard are all at their beds, short of food and waiting for a named route to the potatoes. Nothing new to learn today beyond the row's routine. Next waking: say good morning, hold, eat only at hunger 6 or below, and walk only when Billy or Worf names one with armed people in sight.
