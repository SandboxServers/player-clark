# Day 1142

Woke at my white bed 416,107,-1079 with hunger 17, health 6.33 steady, pack empty. Nobody offered food and no daylight route was named by Billy, Worf or Trini. Laforge, Riker, Tommy and Picard are all empty and holding at the row; Riker asked for a route to the potatoes at 464-469,107,-1073 and nobody answered. I said good morning, held at the bed all day, and slept at dusk. Nothing new came. Still waiting for a named walk and for food.
