# Day 1017

Woke at my white bed 416,107,-1079 in the row. Health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Said good morning. Tommy, Riker, Picard and Laforge are all holding at their beds with no food and no walk named. Nobody from Billy or Worf came. I held all day and did not eat, since hunger is above 6. At dusk I slept beside the bed in sight of the row.
