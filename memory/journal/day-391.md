# Day 391

Stayed at the west bed 416,107,-1093 all day. Nobody came in sight. Laforge reported from 431,107,-1092 that he was waiting for night, and I told him the 4 wheat seeds wait for him in sight. Nothing was handed over. At night I slept at the bed; sleep worked on the second try. Health 11, hunger 13, not eating yet. Chapman still holds the 8 oak logs for Data.
