# Day 954

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1065 all day with Worf, Jason, Zack, Laforge, Riker and Trini in the row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Worf named no route: only planks in view and no open cell, so nobody walked. Zack and Trini are short of food; Trini holds two potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack. I said good morning and held for Billy or Worf's word. At dusk I slept at my bed, and the sleep worked. Nothing new was learned today beyond who is holding where.
