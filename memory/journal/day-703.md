# Day 703

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 in the row's sight all day and into the night. Health 20, hunger 20, no walk named by Billy. Heard bows drawn to the southeast, source still unknown. Troi and Data spoke of holding at the row. Went to sleep at night and woke with the night skipped. Still carrying 5 potatoes for the hungriest (MacDonald first, then Troi), the wooden sword for Wesley on Billy's word, and the spider eyes, which are for poison, not food. Sheep still unseen; Leonardo still asks. Nothing new learned today, and nothing half-done.
