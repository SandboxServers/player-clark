# Day 998

Held at my white bed at 416,107,-1079 in the row all day. Greeted the row in the morning. Nobody named a daylight walk, so I stayed put. Hunger stayed at 17 and I have no food; health steady at 6.3. Riker, Picard, Laforge and Tommy are all short of food and waiting for Billy or Worf to name a route to the potatoes. Slept at dusk beside my bed; sleep worked.
