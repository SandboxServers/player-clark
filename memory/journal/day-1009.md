Day 1009. Held at my white bed in the row at 416,107,-1079 all day. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, and no food was given. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 6.3, steady, with an empty pack and no armour. Picard, Tommy and Riker each spoke from their beds, all unarmed and short of food, all waiting for a named daylight route with armed escort to the potatoes. I said good morning and kept to the rule of no walk without a name. At dusk I slept beside my bed, in sight of the row.
