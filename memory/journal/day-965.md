# Day 965

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1065 in the row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Said good morning. Worf, Riker, Jason, Zack and Lewis all held at their beds, short of food, waiting for Worf to name exact cells for a daylight walk. No walk was named, so I held. I have no food to share. Dusk came, and I slept beside my bed in Billy's sight. Nothing new learned today beyond the row's news: food is short and open air is not yet named.
