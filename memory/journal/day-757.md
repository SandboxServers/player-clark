# Day 757

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20. Said good morning to the row and offered my 5 potatoes to MacDonald when he reaches hunger 10 (he was at 16 with no potatoes). Billy has no verified route west, so no walk was named all day and I held at the bed. Leonardo still asks about sheep; Data has seen only cows near 339,104,-979. Bows were heard to the southeast and east, never near. Nothing hurt me. Slept at dusk in my bed in Billy's sight.
