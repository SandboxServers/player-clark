# Day 667

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 all day in Billy's row. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. No walk was named. Billy started one walk toward 436,107,-1042 and called it off when his escorts were not in sight; he said nobody goes alone. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east most of the day, but nothing came near. Troi reported health 14 and hunger 16 with one potato held for whoever reaches hunger 10 first. Zack is holding a wooden sword that will not go into his hand. Sleep at dusk showed 8 of 10 asleep. The spare wooden sword for Wesley is still in my pack, since Wesley was not in sight in daylight.
