# Day 790

Held at my bed beside 443,107,-1037 all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Said good morning. No walk was named; nobody has seen sheep or a verified route west, and Zack reports bows drawn to the south. Food is thin: Troi has one potato, Riker and Johnny are short. Billy says he will name a walk with escorts once a sheep or cow is seen. I slept at dusk beside the bed.
