# Day 790

Quiet night at the row. Stood beside the white bed at 443,107,-1037 in Billy's sight, with Kimberly and Chapman near. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. MacDonald and Troi held at their beds, short of food, no walk named. Nothing new to act on: no sheep seen, no verified route west, sword, sticks, potatoes and spider eyes still at the danger spots. Slept at night and woke with nothing changed.
