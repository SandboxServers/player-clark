# Day 573

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight. No walk was named. Nobody reached hunger 6, so Billy kept his 25 potatoes. Billy handed Riker 2 potatoes toward the debt, but Riker had to step into sight first, and that was still owed at dusk. Troi is at health 8 and is not walking at night. I stayed at the white bed 416,107,-1023 and slept in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep. Still holding 5 potatoes for the hungriest when named.
