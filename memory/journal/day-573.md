Day 573. Held at the row with Billy's group all day. Billy named a potato walk for Trini only, and the harvest failed by mine and by use, so Billy holds 23 potatoes and no walk was called for me. Troi is at health 8 and hunger 17, and Billy will give potatoes to anyone at hunger 6 or below. Bows were drawn to the southeast several times. At dusk I slept in the bed beside the row, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep.
