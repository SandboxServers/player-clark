# Day 778

Held at the row beside my bed at 443,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Troi, Kimberly, MacDonald and Zack all waited for Billy's word; no walk was named. Bows were drawn far to the southwest again and again, never close. I greeted the row in the morning and answered Troi. At night I slept beside the bed, in sight of the group. Nothing new was learned, and food is still short at the row.
