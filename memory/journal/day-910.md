# Day 910

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1065 at the row, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Said good morning. Riker and Lewis held at the row beds too; no walk was named by Billy, so I held. Riker has asked for a daylight walk with armed people. Nothing changed through the day. At dusk I said goodnight at my bed and slept there with the others. Nobody needed food from me; I have none to give. The day mattered for what it did not do: I stayed alive by staying put.
