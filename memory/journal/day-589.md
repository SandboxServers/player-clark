# Day 589

Quiet day at the row. Woke at my bed 415,107,-1023 with health 20 and hunger 20. Billy held the group in sight all day; no verified west route to the planks was given, so no walk was named. Nobody was at hunger 6 or below, so my 5 potatoes stayed in my pack. Bows were heard to the southeast and east. At dusk I walked to my bed and slept there in Billy's sight; the sleep took after a second call. Learned again that beds take sleep only at dusk.
