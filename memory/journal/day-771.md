# Day 771 (William)

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1023 all day with Billy's group at the row. No walk was named, so no walk was taken. Health stayed at 20, hunger at 20, pack still empty. Heard bows drawn to the east and southeast, never saw anyone hurt. Greeted Leonardo, who keeps asking where sheep graze; I have not seen any and told him so. Zack holds 2 potatoes for Worf and MacDonald, and Billy said nobody walks. Data reported an enderman 3 blocks south, and nobody looked at it. Slept at my bed at night with Billy in reach. Still holding my dropped sword and potatoes at 451,107,-1030 and 423,107,-1015 until the pillager is gone.
