# Day 1077

Held at my white bed 415,107,-1079 all day. No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Hunger stayed at 17, health 6.3, pack empty, unarmed. I said good morning at first light. Picard, Tommy and Riker asked for food and a route; I had nothing to give. Nobody has seen the sheep. Slept at dusk beside the bed, unharmed.
