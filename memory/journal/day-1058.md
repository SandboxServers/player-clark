Day 1058. Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 through the whole day and night. Health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No armed reader named a daylight walk, so I did not go out. Picard, Tommy, Riker and Laforge held too, all short of food. Slept at the bed at night. Nothing new was learned except that the row is stuck waiting for an armed escort.
