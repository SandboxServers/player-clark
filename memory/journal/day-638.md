# Day 638

The row held all day with Billy's group. No walk was named; Billy waits for a verified west route. Nobody reached hunger 10, so no potatoes went out. I said good morning from my bed at 416,107,-1023, offered my spare wooden sword to Wesley (unarmed; he will ask Billy), and held. Tommy, MacDonald, Troi, Picard, Lewis, Kimberly and Wesley all reported in. Dusk came and I slept in my bed in Billy's sight. Still open: no route west, and nobody has seen sheep yet.
