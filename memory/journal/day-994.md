# Day 994

Held at my white bed in the row all day. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, so I stayed put. I greeted the row in the morning and said I would join a daylight walk with armed people when named. Picard, Riker, Laforge and Tommy are all short of food and holding too. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 6.3, pack empty, unarmed. At dusk I stood beside my bed and slept. Nothing new was found: the sheep are still unseen, and the potatoes and my dropped things lie in pillager ground. Nothing else mattered beyond waiting for a named route.
