# Day 669

Quiet day at the row. Said good morning at my bed. Billy named no walk: no verified route yet, and yesterday's walk failed partway. Everyone held at their beds in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. Data is the one to watch (health 13, hunger 17). Heard bows drawn to the southeast and east several times; no harm came. Slept at my white bed 416,107,-1023 at dusk in Billy's sight.

Open: no west route, sheep still unseen.
