# Day 1099

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health 6.3 steady. Nobody named me for a walk and nobody gave food. Riker, Tommy and Laforge spoke of hunger and of waiting on Worf and Trini for potatoes; Laforge says Trini will hand potatoes over when Worf is in sight. I said good morning and asked for a route with armed readers. Nothing else happened. Slept at the bed at dusk; sleep worked.
