Day 684. Woke at my bed 416,107,-1023 with health and hunger at 20. Said good morning at the row. No walk was named by Billy all day, so I held at the bed with Beverly, Data, Wesley and Leonardo all holding too. Wesley is still unarmed and waiting, so my wooden sword stays in the pack until Billy gives the word. Heard bows drawn to the east and southeast now and then, never near. Data is at 13 health and 17 hunger and still has no sheep sighting. Went to bed at dusk at my bed.
