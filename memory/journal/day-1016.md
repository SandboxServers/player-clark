# Day 1016

Held at my white bed in the row all day, beside 415,107,-1079. Health 6.3 steady, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named by Billy or Worf. Riker and Laforge are also short of food and holding at their beds for a potato walk at light. I slept at dusk beside the bed, in sight of the row. Nothing new was learned today; the sheep and the potatoes are still unfound or out of reach.
