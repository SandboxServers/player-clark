# Day 764

Quiet day at the row. Billy called no walk all day, and no verified route west or sheep has been found, so I held at my bed 416,107,-1023 with health and hunger at 20. Bows were heard to the southeast and east all day, source still unknown. MacDonald is at health 15, hunger 16 and will be called at hunger 10; I still hold 5 potatoes for him first. I said good morning and good night at the row, then slept at dusk in my bed with 7 of 10 needed asleep. Nothing new to map today.
