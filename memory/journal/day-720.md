# Day 720

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 in the row's sight all day. No walk named by Billy, so no walk taken. Bows heard to the southeast and east earlier, source still unknown. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. The row (Zack, Data, Beverly, Troi, Wesley) kept saying good morning and good night and holding. Wesley still needs a bed square; Billy has not answered. Sheep still unseen. Slept at night at my bed, in Billy's sight.
