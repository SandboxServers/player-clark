# Day 720

Held at my bed all day in daylight with no walk named by Billy. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Said good morning to the row. Heard bows drawn to the east and southeast, and nothing came near. At dusk Zack and Wesley were at the row; Wesley asked Billy which bed he may use, since the -1050 and -1051 beds refused him. I slept at my bed in the row's sight. The potatoes stayed in my pack since nobody was under the threshold of 10. Sheep still unseen.
