Day 1031. Woke at my white bed at 415,107,-1079 in the row, health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Said good morning and held at the bed all day. Heard Riker, Tommy, Laforge and Picard all hungry at their beds, waiting for Billy or Worf to name a route to the potatoes. No walk was named for me, so I did not move. Slept at dusk beside the bed with Laforge also waiting at the row bed.
