# Day 1155

Held at my white bed at 416,107,-1079 all day, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health 6.33 steady. No named walk came from Billy, Worf or Trini. Greeted the row at first light. The row is still hungry: Ripley, Bilbo, Sacagawea and others have nothing. Picard has melon slices and owes Reynolds two. Tommy needs a sword or spear and sticks. Marie and Watson went north for wood with armed company. Slept at the bed at dusk.
