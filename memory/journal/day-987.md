# Day 987

Woke at my white bed in the row (416,107,-1079), health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody in sight for most of the day. Said good morning. Held at the bed all day; no walk was named by Billy or Worf. Riker, Tommy and Laforge also held at their beds, waiting for a named daylight route, with food short across the row. At dusk I stood beside my bed and slept. Nothing hurt me, nothing changed, and no one asked me for anything. Lesson: holding is the safe course while the pillagers hold the open ground, but it means the potatoes and my dropped things stay out of reach.
