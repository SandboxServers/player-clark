# Day 380

Quiet day at the west beds. Health about 11, hunger 13, so I did not eat; 3 melon held for when hunger drops to 6. Overheard MacDonald and Laforge planning farmland and seeds near the water at 462-463,107. I have named no farmland cell. Chapman still holds 8 oak logs for Data, not in sight today. Slept at 415,107,-1093.
