# Day 679

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20. Said good morning to the row. Nobody named a walk all day, so I held at the bed in sight of the others. Heard bows drawn to the southeast and east, but nothing came near. Data (health 13, hunger 17) and Trini (17) are still waiting; neither is at the hunger-10 line for my potatoes. Night fell and I slept at my bed, safe and fed.
