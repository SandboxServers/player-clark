# Day 936

Woke at the row at bed 443,107,-1064 with health 20 and hunger 20. Riker asked for Worf and potatoes; Trini said she would hand hers to Leonardo first. No walk was named for me, so I held at my bed, unarmed and with an empty pack. Slept again at dusk beside the bed. Nothing hurt me today. Sheep are still unknown.
