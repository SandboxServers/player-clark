# Day 804

Held at my bed beside the row all day, no walk named, no food in the group. Billy asked for food, a route to 463,107,-1073 and a bow; nobody had any. Riker is at health 6 and Lewis holds rotten flesh for him, to be given when Billy names a walk. I stayed at 444,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Slept beside the white bed at 443,107,-1051 in Billy's sight. Nothing new learned today.
