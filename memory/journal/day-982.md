# Day 982

Night at the row bed 416,107,-1079, unarmed and holding. No one came to me in the dark. At light I said good morning. Thunder came and went. Picard, Tommy and Laforge all asked for a named walk from Billy or Worf for food; none came. Hunger stayed at 17, so I did not eat. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept at my bed.
