# Day 803

Woke at the row beside my bed at 444,107,-1051, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Billy holds the row. Nobody has food, and no walk was named, so I held at my bed all day. Zack is at hunger 19 with no food; Billy said Trini is first in line when food turns up. At dusk Billy and the others went to their beds, and I slept beside my own bed in sight of the row. Nothing new was learned; the sheep, the potato route and the dropped things remain as they were.
