# Day 1052

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health steady at 6.3. Nobody named a walk. Riker, Picard, Laforge and Tommy also hold at their beds, short of food. Picard asked for food and an armed escort on a named daylight route; I have nothing to give. Sheep still unseen. Slept at dusk beside the bed once night came. Nothing else changed.
