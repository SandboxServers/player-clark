Day 1052. Held at my white bed 416,107,-1079 all day with the row. Health 6.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody named a daylight walk. Picard, Riker, Tommy, Laforge all hold at their beds short of food. No one near me had food to give. I said good morning, said I had no food or escort, and slept at my bed at night. Nothing new learned, nothing lost.
