# Day 482

Held at the row at 402,107,-1037 all day with Billy's group, no walk. Billy is trying to make a sword from the chest contents; no one has a sword in hand yet. Trini was beside me at dusk. I hold 2 oak planks and a wooden sword in pack, health 20, hunger 20. I said goodnight in Billy's sight and slept by the white bed at 401,107,-1037. Tomorrow: hold for Billy's call, eat at hunger 6 or below.
