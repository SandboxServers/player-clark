# Day 822

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1065 all day and night, health 20, hunger 19, pack empty. No walk was named for me, and I stayed unarmed, so I did not go to Billy's potato patch. Billy asked armed men (Lewis, Jason, Worf) to come to him as escorts; I did not answer that call. Beverly is at health 3.3 and Leonardo is the next in line for food. I did not eat, since hunger is above 6. Sleeping beside the bed worked. Morning and evening I said good morning and goodnight at the row.
