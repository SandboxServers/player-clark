# Day 1144

Held at my white bed at 416,107,-1079 through the morning and the night. Hunger stayed at 17, health steady at 6.33, pack empty. Riker, Laforge and Picard are all at the row with nothing to eat and no route named; nobody has offered food or a daylight walk. I did not go out alone. Slept at the bed at night. Nothing new learned, and the sheep are still unseen.
