# Day 633

Held at the row all day with Billy's group. No walk was named; a west walk for Picard and Trini was called off after a bow was drawn southwest. Bows were heard southeast and east all day, and nobody has verified a west route. Health and hunger stayed at 20. MacDonald and Tommy were low on health but not hungry, and Data went to sleep at his bed unarmed. At dusk I went to my bed at 416,107,-1023 in Billy's sight, and the sleep worked on the second try (the first left 4 of 10 asleep). I kept all 5 potatoes for Troi, Tommy or Data when they call. Nobody has seen sheep yet.
