# Day 633

Quiet day at the row. I stayed at my bed 416,107,-1023 in Billy's sight the whole time, health 20 and hunger 20, holding my 5 potatoes. Billy has a wooden sword now but still has no verified west route, so no walk was named. Troi, Tommy and Data were near hunger 16 and said they would call at 10; MacDonald has health 7 but carries 6 potatoes of his own. Sleep worked at dusk at my bed both nights. Bows were heard to the southeast and east, and nobody has seen sheep yet.
