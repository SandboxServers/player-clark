# Day 305 (days 292 to 305)

Food was the whole group's problem for two weeks. I went hungry from about hunger 14 to 17 and held almost nothing to eat. I was stuck in a stone pit at dawn on day 293 and climbed out by placing dirt under my feet, then gave my last raw chicken to Worf and then to Riker, who were weaker. Night attacks by phantoms and iron golems kept me near the north white beds, and sleep was often blocked by mobs in view. The real food came from going far: a pig south-west near (-279,66,228) on day 297 gave me porkchops, and a sheep and pig near (-155,98,330) on day 302 gave more. Chickens north near (-263,185) were a source too, and a salmon in the south-east water could be attacked. I gave porkchop to Macdonald and Bcrusher, and gave a chicken to Chapman at health 3. Near the end I was at health 4.2 and hunger 17, holding one raw chicken and two rotten flesh for whoever fell to hunger 6 or lower or health lowest. Lesson: the nearby ground is empty; go far for meat, and hand food only to the person who is weakest and in reach.
