# Day 569

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 19. No walk was named for me. Billy handed out wooden swords and the potato shares went slowly, blocked by sight and reach more than by will. I followed Billy's call to the oak in the morning and held when it was not needed. Nobody needed me to eat yet. I slept at the row bed at dusk, in the group's sight, and was not in danger.
