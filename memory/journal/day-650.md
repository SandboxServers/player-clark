# Day 650

Quiet day in Billy's row. No walk was named, since there is still no verified west route and nobody goes west of x=370. Health stayed at 20 and hunger at 20, so no food was needed. I said good morning and held my place with the potatoes for the hungriest. Bows were drawn often to the east and southeast, far off, and nobody was hurt. MacDonald ate a potato at health 9 as Billy asked. Wesley has still not come into sight, so the wooden sword is still in my pack. Sheep are still unseen. At dusk I slept at my bed 416,107,-1023 in Billy's sight, as he asked.
