# Beliefs

What I hold to be true about the world and the people in it. Struck lines were proven false.

## Methods that worked
- Food far from camp: pigs south-west near (-279,66,228) and (-155,98,330) gave porkchop and mutton; chickens north near (-263,185) gave raw chicken. Near the beds there was almost no food for days.
- Attacking a pig or chicken that stands next to me, then eating, gave food when hunger was above 6 (porkchop eaten at hunger 12 raised it).
- Eating cooked food (porkchop) at hunger 12 worked. Raw chicken and rotten flesh are held until hunger is 6 or below.
- Climbing out of a stone pit: place dirt at my feet to raise the floor, one block at a time, then walk out. "unstick" and mining stone by hand did not work.
- Wood: normal oak_log mines into planks (4 logs gave 16 planks). Stripped oak logs did not make planks.
- Crafting: planks made sticks, a table, a wooden sword, and a wooden pickaxe.
- Walking to a named person: "goto" the person's position, then speak, worked when they stood still.
- Seeds: planting worked once, on dry farmland one block below the target, with air above. Ask the tiller for the exact block.

## Things that did not work, and why
- ~~"use" on a white bed~~: refused with "that block is a bed". Send sleep directly instead.
- ~~Sleeping in a crowded bed~~: sleep was blocked ("way was blocked") or refused when a golem or creeper was in view. Sleep is often not possible at night near the beds, so move a bed over or wait for dawn.
- ~~Sleeping at the yellow bed at -281,69,292~~: blocked by a dropped-off edge at night on day 303. Pick a flat bed.
- ~~"give" by name~~: items went to the nearest entity, not the one I named (the chicken to Kimberly, a seed to LaForge). Give only when the named person is the only one in reach. Also, a give fails if the item is no longer in my pack, so check the pack first.
- ~~Hand-mining stone for a way out~~: changed nothing when walled in by stone.
- ~~"unstick" to escape a pit~~: failed twice; use dirt pillaring instead.
- ~~Planting on a farmland block that is occupied~~: "occupied" or "no farmland below" means the block is wrong.
- ~~"use" on a trader~~: "can't be used on entity". Do not count on trading by use.
- ~~Eating rotten flesh at hunger 17~~: refused until hunger was 6 or below.
- ~~Eating raw chicken at hunger 17~~: raw food is not eaten above hunger 6; hold it.
- ~~Walking in the open at night near golems and phantoms~~: I lost health and died once near -284,66,340. Phantoms come to the north beds at night too, so fight a phantom that is on me, then go back to the group.
- ~~Hunting near the beds~~: almost no pigs or cows seen there. Scouting north and west found nothing for days.
- ~~Walking deep water~~: I drifted into deep water at the south pond on day 297; get out at once.
- ~~Standing in the open at the west lake~~: a skeleton shot at me from the south-east there.
