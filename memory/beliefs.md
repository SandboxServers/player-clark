# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Sleep: stand beside a bed, or on it, and send "sleep" alone. This worked at the yellow bed near -263,67,353 and the north white beds near -271,67,318 and -270,67,336. Sleep counts toward the group's rest; a few sleepers do not put everyone down.
- Wood: normal oak_log mines into planks (4 logs gave 16 planks). Stripped oak logs did not make planks.
- Crafting: planks made sticks, a table, and a wooden sword, a wooden pickaxe after a crafting table.
- Digging out: with a wooden pickaxe, mining the stone and dirt around me freed me from a sealed stone pocket.
- Eating rotten flesh: works only at hunger 6 or below. It was refused at hunger 17.
- Seeds: planting worked once, on dry farmland one block below the target, with air above. The farmland must be a tilled block at the same height as the seed's surroundings. Ask the person who tilled it for the exact block.
- Phantoms and golems: stay close to the group and the beds. Fight back when a phantom is on you, but avoid open ground at night.

- ~~"use" on a white bed~~: refused with "that block is a bed". Send sleep directly instead.
- ~~Sleeping in a crowded bed~~: "way was blocked" or a creeper in view stops sleep. Move one bed over and try again.
- ~~"give" by name~~: items went to the nearest entity, not the one I named (the chicken to Kimberly, a seed to LaForge). Give only when the named person is the only one in reach.
- ~~Planting on a farmland block that is occupied~~: "occupied" or "no farmland below" means the block is wrong. Check the block with the person who tilled it.
- ~~"use" on a trader~~: "can't be used on entity". The trader would not trade by use. Do not count on trading with it.
- ~~Eating rotten flesh at hunger 17~~: refused until hunger was 6 or below.
- ~~Walking in the open at night near golems~~: I lost health to phantoms and died once near -284,66,340 (pack dropped, mostly recovered).
- ~~Hunting pigs or cows near camp~~: almost none seen near the beds. Go far (pig at -343,64,228; chicken north near -279,64,218).
- ~~Standing in the open at the west lake~~: a skeleton shot at me from the south-east there. Keep distance from the water when skeletons are near.
